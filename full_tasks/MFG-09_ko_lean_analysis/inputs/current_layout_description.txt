CURRENT PRODUCTION LINE LAYOUT DESCRIPTION
============================================

Factory: Dongguan Precision Manufacturing Co., Ltd.
Product: Automotive body panel sub-assemblies
Line: Main production line #3

LAYOUT TYPE: Functional/Process Layout (NOT cellular)
Total Floor Area: 2,800 m² (70m × 40m)

PROCESS FLOW (left to right):

    [Raw Material Storage] → 12m → [Stamping Area] → 18m → [Welding Zone 1]
                                                              ↓ (8m)
    [Final QC] ← 3m ← [Packaging] ← 8m ← [Inspection]  [Welding Zone 2]
                                                ↑              ↓ (25m)
                                           (5m) |         [Painting Booth]
                                                |              ↓ (3m)
                                        [Assembly 2]      [Drying Oven]
                                            ↑ (20m)           ↓ (15m)
                                        [Assembly 1] ←--------┘

KEY OBSERVATIONS:
- Material flow is NOT linear; multiple backflows and cross-traffic
- Painting booth is located far from assembly (25m transport)
- WIP buffers between most stations (large batch transfers)
- Welding stations are separated despite sequential processing
- Assembly stations are far apart (20m) despite being sequential
- No clear one-piece-flow implementation
- Forklift paths cross pedestrian walkways in 3 locations

MATERIAL HANDLING:
- Forklifts: 3 units, constant movement
- Hand carts: 5 units
- Conveyor: Only between Drying → Assembly 1 (15m, roller conveyor)
- All other transport is manual/forklift

STORAGE AREAS:
- Raw material: 200 m² (contains ~3 days of stock)
- WIP staging areas: 5 locations, total 180 m²
- Finished goods: 150 m² (holds ~2 days production)

SHIFT PATTERN:
- 2 shifts × 8 hours = 16 hours/day production
- 30 min meal break per shift
- Planned maintenance: 30 min/day (during shift change)
- Effective production time: 480 min/shift

CURRENT ISSUES NOTED BY MANAGEMENT:
1. Cannot meet customer demand of 480 units/day (current: ~400)
2. High WIP inventory (815 pieces across line)
3. Welding is identified bottleneck
4. Long changeover times especially at painting
5. Quality issues at painting station (4% defect rate)
6. Excessive transport distances
7. Workers complain about ergonomic issues at assembly stations
