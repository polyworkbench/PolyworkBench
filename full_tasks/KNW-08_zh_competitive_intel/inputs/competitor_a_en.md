## Press Release: NovaTech Unveils Nova-7 AI Accelerator (October 15, 2024)

**SAN JOSE, CA** — NovaTech Inc. today announced the Nova-7, its next-generation AI training accelerator chip, delivering unprecedented performance for large language model training and inference workloads.

### Key Specifications:
- **Process Node:** TSMC 3nm
- **Transistor Count:** 128 billion
- **HBM3E Memory:** 192 GB per chip
- **Memory Bandwidth:** 9.6 TB/s
- **TDP:** 700W
- **FP8 Performance:** 3,200 TFLOPS
- **FP16 Performance:** 1,600 TFLOPS
- **Interconnect:** NovaBridge 4.0 (900 GB/s chip-to-chip)

### Pricing and Availability:
- **MSRP:** $32,000 per chip
- **System Price (8-chip node):** $380,000
- **Availability:** Q1 2025 (sampling), Q2 2025 (volume production)
- **Initial Customers:** Three hyperscale cloud providers (unnamed), two sovereign AI programs

### Performance Claims:
- 2.4x training throughput improvement over Nova-6 for GPT-4 class models
- 40% reduction in energy consumption per token for inference workloads
- Supports models up to 5 trillion parameters in a single 256-chip cluster

## Press Release: NovaTech Secures $4.2 Billion in Design Wins (November 8, 2024)

NovaTech announced $4.2 billion in confirmed design wins for 2025-2026 delivery, including:
- **$1.8 billion** from a leading US hyperscaler for AI training infrastructure
- **$1.1 billion** from a Middle Eastern sovereign AI initiative
- **$800 million** from European automotive OEMs for autonomous driving compute
- **$500 million** from Japanese enterprise customers

The company also announced a partnership with **Samsung Foundry** as a secondary manufacturing source for its Nova-7 chip, reducing TSMC concentration risk.

## Press Release: NovaTech Opens R&D Center in Tel Aviv (September 22, 2024)

NovaTech announced the opening of a new AI systems research center in Tel Aviv, Israel, with an initial team of 180 engineers focused on:
- Next-generation compiler optimization for large model training
- Novel memory architectures for inference efficiency
- Automated chip design using AI

**Investment:** $120 million over 3 years  
**Hiring target:** 500 engineers by end of 2026
