# Rubric: KNW-08

## Dimension 1: Competitive Digest Quality (Weight: 25%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Chinese competitive digest covering all competitors (NovaTech, SiliconWave, QuantumCore) with analysis of strategies, strengths, weaknesses
- **Good (7-8)**: Chinese digest covering most competitors
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Basic competitor coverage
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal competitive content
- **Zero (0)**: No competitive digest

## Dimension 2: Chinese Language Quality (Weight: 25%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Professional Chinese (>15% CJK ratio) in digest and strategic implications, proper business/tech terminology
- **Good (7-8)**: Good Chinese content with terminology
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Some Chinese content
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal Chinese
- **Zero (0)**: No Chinese content

## Dimension 3: Threat Analysis (Weight: 20%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Structured threat analysis with severity levels, timeline, and specific competitive threats mapped to business impact
- **Good (7-8)**: Threat analysis with structure and some mapping
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Basic threat identification
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal threat content
- **Zero (0)**: No threat analysis

## Dimension 4: Strategic Implications (Weight: 15%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Chinese strategic implications document (300+ chars) with actionable recommendations for response to competitive threats
- **Good (7-8)**: Strategic content with some recommendations
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Basic strategic observations
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal strategic content
- **Zero (0)**: No strategic implications

## Dimension 5: Competitor Profiles (Weight: 15%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Structured profiles with consistent schema for each competitor, quantitative data, technology positioning
- **Good (7-8)**: Profiles with most data points
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Basic profiles
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal profile data
- **Zero (0)**: No profiles

## Language Quality Assessment
- Target language output must be professional quality Chinese
- No English fallback in competitive digest and strategic implications
- Technical terminology must be domain-appropriate for competitive intelligence
