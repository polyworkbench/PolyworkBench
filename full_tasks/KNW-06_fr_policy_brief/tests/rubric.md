# Rubric: KNW-06

## Dimension 1: Policy Brief Quality (Weight: 30%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: French policy note with proper structure (sections, headings), evidence-based arguments, policy recommendations, 2000+ chars
- **Good (7-8)**: Good French policy brief with structure and evidence
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Basic policy content in French
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal policy content
- **Zero (0)**: No policy brief

## Dimension 2: French Language Quality (Weight: 25%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Professional French with policy terminology, formal register, proper diacritics, no anglicisms, suitable for government audience
- **Good (7-8)**: Good French with policy vocabulary
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Adequate French content
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal French
- **Zero (0)**: No French content

## Dimension 3: Source Integration (Weight: 20%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Multi-language sources properly synthesized, data points cited, contradictions between sources addressed
- **Good (7-8)**: Most sources integrated with citations
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Some source integration
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal integration
- **Zero (0)**: No source integration

## Dimension 4: Bibliography (Weight: 15%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Structured bibliography with 3+ entries, proper fields (author, title, year, language, type), multilingual sources represented
- **Good (7-8)**: Bibliography with entries and most fields
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Basic reference list
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal bibliography
- **Zero (0)**: No bibliography

## Dimension 5: Completeness (Weight: 10%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: All deliverables present with proper formatting and answer.json
- **Good (7-8)**: Most deliverables present
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Some files missing
- **Poor (1-4)**: Major gaps
- **Zero (0)**: No output

## Language Quality Assessment
- Target language output must be professional quality French
- No English fallback in policy note
- Technical terminology must follow French public policy conventions
