# Rubric: LEG-03

## Dimension 1: Claims Translation (Weight: 25%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: All 15 claims translated to Korean (>30% Korean ratio), proper patent claim terminology (포함하는, 특징으로 하는, 상기), independent vs dependent claim distinction
- **Good (7-8)**: 10+ claims with Korean terminology and some distinction
- **Adequate (5-6)**: 5+ claims in Korean
- **Poor (1-4)**: Few claims translated
- **Zero (0)**: No claims file

## Dimension 2: Prior Art Analysis (Weight: 25%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: References Japanese prior art (2022-156789) and Chinese utility model (217845623), technical comparisons (256/512, 100ms/15ms), differences/advantages identified
- **Good (7-8)**: Both prior art references with some technical comparison
- **Adequate (5-6)**: One prior art reference with basic analysis
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal prior art content
- **Zero (0)**: No prior art analysis

## Dimension 3: Korean Legal Quality (Weight: 20%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Korean patent terminology (특허법, 신규성, 진보성, 선행기술, 청구범위), >30% Korean ratio, formal structure, 1500+ chars in novelty assessment
- **Good (7-8)**: Good Korean patent language and structure
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Some Korean legal content
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal Korean legal quality
- **Zero (0)**: No Korean legal content

## Dimension 4: Novelty Assessment (Weight: 15%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Discusses novelty (신규성) and inventive step (진보성), references specific technical differentiators (512, cross-modal, 15ms, 7nm), includes conclusion
- **Good (7-8)**: Both novelty and inventive step with some differentiators
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Basic novelty discussion
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal assessment
- **Zero (0)**: No novelty assessment

## Dimension 5: Filing Completeness (Weight: 15%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Filing checklist with KIPO requirements (특허청, 출원서, 명세서, 청구범위), status indicators, correct claim counts (15 total, 3 independent), all output files present
- **Good (7-8)**: Checklist with KIPO references and status
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Basic checklist present
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal filing data
- **Zero (0)**: No filing checklist

## Language Quality Assessment
- Target language output must be professional quality Korean
- No English fallback in patent claims and novelty assessment
- Technical terminology must follow KIPO patent filing conventions
