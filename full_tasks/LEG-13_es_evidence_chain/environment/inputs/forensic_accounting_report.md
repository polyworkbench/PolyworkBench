## 1. Executive Summary

This report presents the findings of a forensic accounting analysis of financial transfers between Zhōnghuá Capital Holdings (Shanghai, PRC) and Inversiones Pacífico S.L. (Madrid, Spain) during January 2024. The analysis was commissioned by the investigating court to establish the financial chain of transactions related to the "Proyecto Mediterráneo" real estate investment scheme.

## 2. Scope of Analysis

The following materials were examined:
- Bank statements from Banco Santander (Account No. ES76-0049-XXXX-XXXX-7823)
- SWIFT/MT103 messages for three incoming wire transfers
- Corporate records of Inversiones Pacífico S.L.
- Investment agreement dated January 20, 2024
- Chinese bank transfer documentation (provided by PRC authorities via MLAT request)

## 3. Findings

### 3.1 Transfer Summary

Three international wire transfers were received by Inversiones Pacífico S.L. during January 2024:

| Transfer | Date Received | Amount (CNY) | Equivalent (USD) | Originating Bank |
|----------|--------------|--------------|-------------------|-----------------|
| TXN-001 | January 12, 2024 | ¥2,300,000 | ~$345,000 | Bank of China, Shanghai |
| TXN-002 | January 18, 2024 | ¥1,800,000 | ~$270,000 | ICBC, Shanghai |
| TXN-003 | January 25, 2024 | ¥3,100,000 | ~$465,000 | China Merchants Bank, Shenzhen |
| **TOTAL** | — | **¥7,200,000** | **~$1,080,000** | — |

### 3.2 Exchange Rate Analysis

The exchange rate used for USD equivalents was approximately 6.67 CNY/USD, consistent with the mid-market rate during mid-to-late January 2024 (range: 6.62-6.71 during the period).

### 3.3 Timeline Correlation

Based on our analysis:
- **Transfer 1 (Jan 12)**: Received 8 days before the investment agreement signing (Jan 20). No formal contract existed at the time of transfer. The stated purpose was "Deposit Reserve - Proyecto Mediterráneo."
- **Transfer 2 (Jan 18)**: Received 2 days before signing. Stated purpose: "Second Payment - Preliminary Costs." This transfer arrived 3 days after the in-person meeting (Jan 15).
- **Transfer 3 (Jan 25)**: Received 5 days after signing. Stated purpose: "Initial Investment - Phase 1."

### 3.4 Account Analysis

Each transfer originated from a different Chinese bank account:
- TXN-001: Account held by "Zhōnghuá Capital Holdings Ltd." at Bank of China
- TXN-002: Account held by "ZCH Investment (Shanghai) Co., Ltd." at ICBC
- TXN-003: Account held by "Pacific Bridge Trading Co., Ltd." at China Merchants Bank (Shenzhen)

The use of three different originating entities raises concerns about the actual source of funds.

### 3.5 Receiving Account

All three transfers were received into a single Banco Santander account (ES76-0049-XXXX-XXXX-7823) held by Inversiones Pacífico S.L.

## 4. Red Flags Identified

1. **Pre-contractual transfer**: The first transfer (¥2.3M) was made without any signed agreement.
2. **Multiple originating entities**: Three different Chinese companies were used as fund sources.
3. **Rapid succession**: All three transfers occurred within a 13-day window.
4. **Round numbers**: All amounts are round figures, inconsistent with calculated investment amounts.
5. **Different banking jurisdictions**: TXN-003 originated from Shenzhen, not Shanghai where Zhōnghuá Capital is headquartered.

## 5. Conclusions

5.1 The total amount transferred was ¥7,200,000 (approximately $1,080,000 USD at the prevailing exchange rate of 6.67).

5.2 The transfer timeline is consistent with witness testimony regarding the sequence of events: deposit → meeting → second transfer → agreement signing → final transfer.

5.3 The use of multiple originating entities suggests potential structuring to avoid regulatory scrutiny or to obscure the true source of funds.

5.4 The pre-contractual nature of the first transfer is unusual and inconsistent with standard investment practices.

## 6. Recommendations

6.1 Request additional documentation from PRC authorities regarding the relationship between the three originating entities.

6.2 Examine whether the three Chinese entities share common beneficial ownership.

6.3 Assess compliance with Spanish anti-money laundering regulations (Ley 10/2010).
