# Customs Clearance Timelines

## Russia (Import from China)

### Standard Commercial Shipments
- **Document preparation**: 2-3 business days
- **Customs declaration filing**: 1 business day
- **Customs inspection (if selected)**: 1-3 business days
- **Duty payment processing**: 1-2 business days
- **Release after clearance**: Same day or next business day

**Total typical timeline**: 5-9 business days

### Express/Courier Shipments (under $200 value)
- **Simplified declaration**: 1 business day
- **Automated clearance**: Same day
- **Total**: 1-2 business days

### Key Duties & Taxes (Russia)
- Import duty: 0-20% depending on HS code
- VAT: 20% on (CIF value + duty)
- Electronics (HS 8471-8473): 0% duty
- Consumer electronics (HS 8517-8518): 5% duty
- Textiles/clothing: 10-15% duty
- Personal imports over ₽10,000: 15% duty + VAT

### Common Delays
- Incomplete documentation: +3-5 days
- Random inspection: +1-3 days
- Valuation dispute: +5-10 days
- Sanctioned goods check: +2-4 days

## Vietnam (Import from China)

### Standard Commercial Shipments
- **Document preparation**: 1-2 business days
- **Customs declaration filing**: 1 business day (electronic)
- **Green channel (low risk)**: Same day release
- **Yellow channel (document review)**: 1-2 business days
- **Red channel (physical inspection)**: 2-4 business days

**Total typical timeline**: 2-5 business days

### Express/Courier Shipments
- **Simplified procedure (under $100)**: Same day
- **Standard express**: 1 business day
- **Total**: 0-1 business days

### Key Duties & Taxes (Vietnam)
- Import duty: 0-30% depending on HS code
- VAT: 10% (standard rate)
- Electronics components (HS 8542): 0% duty (ACFTA)
- Consumer electronics (HS 8517): 0-5% duty (ACFTA)
- Finished goods: 10-20% duty
- ACFTA preferential rate available for qualifying goods with Form E

### Common Delays
- Missing Form E (certificate of origin): +2-3 days
- HS code classification dispute: +3-5 days
- Quarantine inspection (if applicable): +2-3 days
- Port congestion (seasonal): +1-3 days
