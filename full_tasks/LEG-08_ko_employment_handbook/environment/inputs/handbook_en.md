# Global Employee Handbook

## Company: TechNova Inc.
## Version: 3.2 | Effective Date: January 1, 2024

---

## 1. Employment Classification

### 1.1 Employee Types
- Full-time: 40+ hours per week
- Part-time: Less than 40 hours per week
- Temporary: Fixed-term employment up to 12 months
- Contractor: Independent contractor (not an employee)

### 1.2 At-Will Employment
Employment with TechNova is "at-will." Either the employee or the company may terminate the employment relationship at any time, with or without cause, and with or without notice.

### 1.3 Probationary Period
New employees are subject to a 90-day probationary period during which performance will be evaluated.

---

## 2. Working Hours

### 2.1 Standard Work Week
The standard work week is 40 hours, Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM with a 1-hour lunch break.

### 2.2 Overtime
Non-exempt employees will be compensated at 1.5x their regular rate for hours worked in excess of 40 per week. Overtime must be pre-approved by your manager.

### 2.3 Flexible Working
Employees may request flexible working arrangements including remote work, compressed work weeks, or adjusted start/end times, subject to manager approval.

---

## 3. Compensation & Benefits

### 3.1 Pay Periods
Employees are paid bi-weekly (every two weeks) via direct deposit.

### 3.2 Annual Bonus
Eligible employees may receive an annual performance bonus of 0-20% of base salary, based on individual and company performance.

### 3.3 Health Insurance
The company provides medical, dental, and vision insurance. Coverage begins on the first day of the month following 30 days of employment.

### 3.4 Retirement Plan
The company offers a 401(k) retirement plan with a company match of up to 4% of salary.

---

## 4. Paid Time Off (PTO)

### 4.1 Annual Leave
- Years 1-3: 15 days per year
- Years 4-7: 20 days per year
- Years 8+: 25 days per year

### 4.2 Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year. Unused sick days do not carry over.

### 4.3 Personal Days
Employees receive 3 personal days per year.

### 4.4 PTO Accrual
PTO accrues on a monthly basis. Maximum carryover is 5 days to the following year.

---

## 5. Leave of Absence

### 5.1 Maternity Leave
Birth mothers are entitled to 12 weeks of paid maternity leave at 100% salary.

### 5.2 Paternity Leave
Fathers/partners are entitled to 4 weeks of paid paternity leave.

### 5.3 Family Medical Leave
Up to 12 weeks of unpaid leave per year for qualifying family medical reasons (FMLA).

### 5.4 Bereavement Leave
- Immediate family: 5 days paid
- Extended family: 3 days paid

### 5.5 Jury Duty
Full pay for up to 10 days of jury duty per year.

---

## 6. Holidays

### 6.1 Company Holidays
The company observes 10 paid holidays per year:
- New Year's Day (January 1)
- Martin Luther King Jr. Day
- Presidents' Day
- Memorial Day
- Independence Day (July 4)
- Labor Day
- Columbus Day
- Veterans Day
- Thanksgiving Day
- Christmas Day (December 25)

---

## 7. Performance Management

### 7.1 Annual Reviews
All employees receive an annual performance review in Q1 of each year.

### 7.2 Goal Setting
Employees set annual goals in January with their manager using the OKR framework.

### 7.3 Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
Employees whose performance is below expectations may be placed on a 60-day PIP.

---

## 8. Termination

### 8.1 Voluntary Resignation
Employees are requested to provide at least 2 weeks' notice of resignation.

### 8.2 Involuntary Termination
The company may terminate employment at any time for any lawful reason. Where possible, the company will provide 2 weeks' notice or pay in lieu of notice.

### 8.3 Severance
Employees with 2+ years of service who are terminated without cause may receive severance equal to 2 weeks per year of service, up to 26 weeks.

---

## 9. Anti-Discrimination & Harassment

### 9.1 Equal Opportunity
TechNova is an equal opportunity employer. We do not discriminate on the basis of race, color, religion, sex, national origin, age, disability, or any other protected characteristic.

### 9.2 Anti-Harassment Policy
Harassment of any kind is strictly prohibited. This includes sexual harassment, bullying, and creating a hostile work environment.

### 9.3 Reporting Procedure
Employees should report any incidents to HR or use the anonymous hotline.

---

## 10. Code of Conduct

### 10.1 Professional Behavior
Employees are expected to maintain professional behavior at all times.

### 10.2 Confidentiality
Employees must not disclose proprietary or confidential information.

### 10.3 Conflict of Interest
Employees must disclose any potential conflicts of interest to their manager.

### 10.4 Social Media
Employees should not post company confidential information on social media.

---

## 11. Workplace Safety

### 11.1 Safety Standards
The company maintains a safe work environment in compliance with OSHA regulations.

### 11.2 Incident Reporting
All workplace injuries must be reported immediately to the safety officer.

### 11.3 Emergency Procedures
Emergency evacuation procedures are posted in all office locations.

---

## 12. Remote Work Policy

### 12.1 Eligibility
All full-time employees who have completed their probationary period may request remote work.

### 12.2 Equipment
The company provides a laptop and monitor for remote workers. Internet costs are reimbursed up to $75/month.

### 12.3 Availability
Remote workers must be available during core hours (10:00 AM - 4:00 PM local time).

---

## 13. Training & Development

### 13.1 Education Reimbursement
Up to $5,000 per year for approved courses and certifications.

### 13.2 Internal Training
Employees are expected to complete mandatory compliance training annually.

---

## 14. Expense Policy

### 14.1 Travel
All business travel must be pre-approved. Economy class for flights under 6 hours.

### 14.2 Entertainment
Client entertainment expenses up to $100 per person require manager approval; over $100 requires VP approval.

---

## 15. Intellectual Property

### 15.1 Work Product
All work product created during employment belongs to the company.

### 15.2 Inventions
Employees must disclose all inventions made during employment using company resources.

---

## 16. Data Privacy

### 16.1 Employee Data
The company collects and processes employee personal data in accordance with applicable privacy laws.

### 16.2 Customer Data
Employees must handle customer data in accordance with the company's data protection policy.

---

## 17. Disciplinary Procedures

### 17.1 Progressive Discipline
1. Verbal warning
2. Written warning
3. Final written warning
4. Termination

### 17.2 Immediate Termination
Gross misconduct may result in immediate termination without progressive discipline.

---

## 18. Grievance Procedure

### 18.1 Filing a Grievance
Employees may file a grievance with HR within 30 days of the incident.

### 18.2 Resolution
HR will investigate and respond within 15 business days.

---

## 19. Drug & Alcohol Policy

### 19.1 Prohibition
The use or possession of drugs or alcohol on company premises is prohibited.

### 19.2 Testing
The company reserves the right to conduct drug testing where permitted by law.

---

## 20. Dress Code

### 20.1 Business Casual
The standard dress code is business casual. Client-facing meetings may require business formal attire.
