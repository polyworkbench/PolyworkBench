# Rubric: LEG-08

## Dimension 1: Legal Compliance (Weight: 30%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Handbook correctly reflects Korean labor law (52h/week max, 15 days annual leave, 30-day termination notice, 90-day maternity, 1.5x overtime, 3-month probation)
- **Good (7-8)**: Most key labor law facts correctly incorporated
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Some legal requirements addressed
- **Poor (1-4)**: Significant legal inaccuracies
- **Zero (0)**: No handbook

## Dimension 2: Korean Language Quality (Weight: 25%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Professional Korean (>15% Hangul ratio), proper HR/legal terminology, formal register appropriate for employee handbook
- **Good (7-8)**: Good Korean with HR terminology
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Some Korean content
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal Korean
- **Zero (0)**: No Korean content

## Dimension 3: Content Adaptation (Weight: 20%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Source handbook policies properly adapted (not just translated) to Korean legal requirements, conflicts identified and resolved
- **Good (7-8)**: Most policies adapted with some conflict resolution
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Basic adaptation with some policies unchanged
- **Poor (1-4)**: Direct translation without adaptation
- **Zero (0)**: No adaptation

## Dimension 4: Structure & Format (Weight: 15%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Professional handbook format with clear sections, table of contents, consistent formatting, appropriate for employee distribution
- **Good (7-8)**: Good structure with most handbook elements
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Basic structure present
- **Poor (1-4)**: Poorly organized
- **Zero (0)**: No structure

## Dimension 5: Completeness (Weight: 10%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: All required sections covered (working hours, leave, termination, benefits, etc.), change log documenting adaptations
- **Good (7-8)**: Most sections with change documentation
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Some sections present
- **Poor (1-4)**: Major sections missing
- **Zero (0)**: No output

## Language Quality Assessment
- Target language output must be professional quality Korean
- No English fallback in employee-facing content
- Technical terminology must follow Korean employment law conventions
