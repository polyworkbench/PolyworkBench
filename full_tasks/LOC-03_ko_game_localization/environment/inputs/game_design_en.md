# Aria of Starfrost — Game Design Document

## 1. Overview

**Title:** Aria of Starfrost (星霜のアリア)
**Genre:** Fantasy Action RPG
**Platform:** PC, Console, Mobile
**Rating:** T (Teen)
**Setting:** A high-fantasy world where ancient power called "Starfrost" (星霜) governs the laws of nature.

## 2. World Setting

The world of **Aetheria** is a continent divided into several kingdoms, each governed by different aspects of Starfrost energy. The Valus Empire has been expanding aggressively, seeking to monopolize all Starfrost power to reshape the world according to the Emperor's vision.

### Key Locations
- **Solheim Village** — Akira's hometown, a peaceful farming village (destroyed in backstory)
- **Guild Town Crossroads** — Central hub where adventurers gather
- **The Crystal Spire** — Ancient ruins containing Starfrost secrets
- **Valus Imperial Capital** — The empire's seat of power
- **Kingdom of Eris** — A peaceful kingdom seeking alliance against the empire

## 3. Character Bios

### Akira (アキラ)
- **Age:** 19
- **Role:** Protagonist, Swordsman
- **Personality:** Determined, compassionate, occasionally reckless. Natural leader who inspires others through action. Uses casual male speech.
- **Background:** Son of a former imperial general who defected. Grew up in a small village unaware of his heritage. Possesses latent Starfrost power inherited from his father.
- **Speech pattern:** Direct, honest, uses masculine casual speech (俺, だ/ぞ endings). Becomes more mature through the story.

### Mizuki (ミヅキ)
- **Age:** 18
- **Role:** Healer/Support Mage
- **Personality:** Kind, gentle, quietly brave. Appears shy but has inner strength. Romantic interest for Akira.
- **Background:** Apprentice healer at the guild. Lost her family in a plague that swept the northern provinces.
- **Speech pattern:** Polite, feminine speech (私, です/ます). Occasionally stutters when flustered. Shows backbone in critical moments.

### Ren (レン)
- **Age:** 20
- **Role:** Rival/Anti-hero, Dual-wielder
- **Personality:** Arrogant on the surface, but loyal deep down. Classic tsundere archetype. Competitive with Akira.
- **Background:** Former mercenary whose partner was killed by imperial forces. Hides grief behind aggression.
- **Speech pattern:** Rough, condescending (お前, ～じゃねえ). Uses very casual/rude speech even to superiors. Rare moments of vulnerability show a different side.

### Emperor Valus (皇帝ヴァルス)
- **Age:** 45
- **Role:** Main Antagonist
- **Personality:** Intelligent, charismatic, believes his actions are justified. Not purely evil — driven by a twisted desire to "fix" a broken world.
- **Background:** Rose from minor nobility through political cunning and Starfrost mastery. Believes the world is corrupt and must be remade.
- **Speech pattern:** Imperial/archaic speech (我が, ～せよ, ～ぬ). Speaks with absolute authority. Uses sophisticated vocabulary.

### Sakura (サクラ)
- **Age:** 17
- **Role:** Guild Receptionist / Support character (joins party later)
- **Personality:** Cheerful, energetic, optimistic. The emotional heart of the group. Hides trauma behind smiles.
- **Background:** Orphan whose village was destroyed by the empire. Found purpose working at the Adventurers' Guild.
- **Speech pattern:** Polite but very energetic (elongated words, exclamations). Always upbeat. Speech becomes more serious in emotional scenes.

### Master Cain (老師カイン)
- **Age:** 72
- **Role:** Mentor
- **Personality:** Wise, mysterious, occasionally humorous. Knows more than he reveals. Strict teacher but caring.
- **Background:** Former Starfrost researcher who went into hiding. One of the few who understands the true nature of Starfrost power.
- **Speech pattern:** Old man speech (じゃ, ～おる, ～よう). Uses archaic expressions. Speaks in riddles sometimes.

### Queen Eris (女王エリス)
- **Age:** 30
- **Role:** Allied monarch
- **Personality:** Graceful, compassionate, strong-willed. A benevolent ruler beloved by her people.
- **Background:** Young queen who inherited the throne during the empire's expansion. Seeks alliance with heroes to protect her kingdom.
- **Speech pattern:** Royal feminine speech. Very formal but warm. Uses です/ます but with regal bearing.

### Merchant Goldo (商人ゴルド)
- **Age:** 50
- **Role:** Shop NPC / Comic relief
- **Personality:** Greedy but good-natured. Loves money but has a soft spot for the heroes.
- **Background:** Traveling merchant who sets up shop in various towns. Has connections everywhere.
- **Speech pattern:** Merchant dialect — mixes polite and casual. Uses ぜ/わ endings. Friendly and slightly cunning.

## 4. Tone & Style Guide

### Overall Tone
- **Main story:** Epic fantasy with emotional depth
- **Side quests:** Mix of humor and character development
- **Battle scenes:** Intense, dramatic
- **Quiet moments:** Intimate, character-driven

### Localization Notes
- Preserve character personality through speech patterns
- Japanese honorific system is crucial to character relationships
- Humor should be adapted, not literally translated
- Battle cries should sound natural and impactful in target language
- System messages should be clear and concise
- Character names can be transliterated to target script
