# Brand Guidelines — GlobalTrend Commerce

## Brand Voice
- Professional yet approachable
- Emphasize quality and value proposition
- Use local cultural references when possible
- Avoid direct translation of Chinese marketing clichés

## Naming Convention
- Product names should be descriptive (2-5 words max for title)
- Include key material/feature in subtitle
- Never use "cheap" or "budget" — use "affordable" or "value"

## Required Listing Fields (Ozon marketplace)
1. Title (max 150 chars)
2. Description (500-2000 chars)
3. Category
4. Brand
5. SKU
6. Price (RUB)
7. Size chart (if applicable)
8. Material/Composition
9. Country of origin
10. Certification marks (EAC/TR TS if applicable)
11. Weight & dimensions
12. SEO keywords (5-8 per product)
13. Images (not provided — skip)

## Pricing Strategy
- Apply 2.8x markup from factory price
- Round to nearest 9 (e.g., 1499, 2999)
- Electronics: 2.2x markup
- Food: 3.5x markup (import costs higher)

## Compliance Notes
- All textile products need TR TS 017/2011 marking
- Electronics need TR TS 004/2011 + TR TS 020/2011
- Food products need TR TS 021/2011 + TR TS 022/2011
- Baby products need TR TS 007/2011
- Cosmetics need TR TS 009/2011
