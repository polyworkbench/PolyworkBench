## 1. Introduction

CloudMetrics Inc. ("CloudMetrics", "we", "us", "our") provides a cloud-based analytics and business intelligence platform (the "Service") to enterprise customers worldwide. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, disclose, and protect personal data when you use our Service.

This policy applies to all users of our platform, including visitors to our website, trial users, paying customers, and their end users whose data may be processed through our platform.

---

## 2. Data We Collect

### 2.1 Account Data
- Name, email address, phone number
- Company name, job title, business address
- Payment information (credit card, billing address)
- Account credentials (hashed passwords)

### 2.2 Usage Data
- IP addresses, browser type, device information
- Pages visited, features used, time spent on platform
- Clickstream data, scroll depth, mouse movements
- Search queries within the platform

### 2.3 Customer Data (Processed on Behalf of Customers)
- End-user behavioral data uploaded by our customers
- Customer database exports for analytics processing
- CRM data, purchase history, user demographics
- Health data, financial data, or other sensitive categories as uploaded by customers

### 2.4 Data from Third Parties
- Enrichment data from data brokers (demographic profiles, interests, purchasing behavior)
- Social media profiles (LinkedIn, Twitter) for user identification
- Credit scoring data for payment risk assessment

## 3. How We Use Your Data

### 3.1 Service Delivery
- Providing and maintaining the analytics platform
- Processing customer data according to customer instructions
- Generating analytics reports and dashboards

### 3.2 Service Improvement
- Analyzing usage patterns to improve platform features
- Training machine learning models on aggregated customer data to improve prediction accuracy
- Automated profiling of user behavior to provide personalized recommendations and predictive analytics

### 3.3 Marketing
- Sending promotional emails about new features and services
- Displaying targeted advertisements based on usage patterns
- Creating lookalike audiences for advertising purposes

### 3.4 Legal and Compliance
- Complying with legal obligations
- Responding to law enforcement requests
- Protecting our rights and preventing fraud

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data based on:
- **Performance of contract** (Account Data, Service Delivery)
- **Legitimate interest** (Service Improvement, Marketing, Profiling, ML training)
- **Consent** (cookies, marketing emails)
- **Legal obligation** (compliance, law enforcement)

For automated profiling and ML model training, we rely on legitimate interest. Users may object to profiling by contacting support@cloudmetrics.io.

---

## 5. Data Retention

- Account data: retained for the duration of the account plus 7 years after account closure
- Usage data: retained for 5 years from collection
- Customer data: retained for 3 years after contract termination
- Marketing data: retained indefinitely until opt-out
- Server logs: retained for 24 months
- ML training datasets: retained indefinitely (aggregated and pseudonymized)

---

## 6. International Data Transfers

CloudMetrics processes data in the following locations:
- **Primary processing:** United States (AWS us-east-1, us-west-2)
- **Backup/DR:** United States (AWS us-east-2)
- **Analytics processing:** United States (Google Cloud us-central1)
- **Customer support:** Philippines (outsourced support center)

For transfers of EU/EEA personal data to the United States, we rely on:
- Standard Contractual Clauses (2021 version)
- Supplementary measures as described in our Transfer Impact Assessment

We participate in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and have self-certified with the Department of Commerce.

**Note:** Following the Schrems II ruling, we have not completed individual Transfer Impact Assessments for each data flow. We rely on the general adequacy of our supplementary measures as a whole.

## 7. Data Sharing

We share personal data with:
- **Cloud infrastructure providers:** AWS, Google Cloud
- **Analytics sub-processors:** Segment, Mixpanel, Amplitude
- **Advertising partners:** Google Ads, Facebook/Meta, LinkedIn Marketing
- **Data enrichment providers:** Clearbit, ZoomInfo

Sub-processor agreements are in place with all third-party data processors. A complete sub-processor list is available upon request.

## 8. Cookies and Tracking

We use the following cookie categories:
- **Essential cookies:** Required for platform functionality
- **Analytics cookies:** Google Analytics, Hotjar (loaded on page visit)
- **Marketing cookies:** Facebook Pixel, Google Ads tag, LinkedIn Insight Tag

Our cookie banner provides two options: "Accept All" or "Continue to Site." Selecting "Continue to Site" still loads analytics cookies as they are classified as necessary for service improvement.

## 9. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, you have the right to:
- Access your personal data
- Rectify inaccurate data
- Erase your data (subject to legal retention requirements)
- Restrict processing
- Data portability
- Object to processing based on legitimate interest

To exercise these rights, please email privacy@cloudmetrics.io. We will respond within 45 days (extendable to 90 days for complex requests).

## 10. Automated Decision-Making

We use automated profiling to:
- Predict customer churn risk
- Recommend platform features
- Determine pricing tiers for new customers
- Assess credit risk for payment terms

These automated decisions may have significant effects on users, including pricing differences of up to 40% between user segments. Users may request human review of automated decisions.
