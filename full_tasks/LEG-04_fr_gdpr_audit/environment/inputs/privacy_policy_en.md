# Privacy Policy

**CloudMetrics Analytics SaaS Platform**
**Last Updated:** January 10, 2024
**Version:** 4.2

## 1. Introduction

CloudMetrics Analytics ("CloudMetrics", "we", "us", or "our") provides a cloud-based analytics platform that helps businesses understand user behavior on their websites and applications. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, share, and protect personal data when you use our services.

CloudMetrics is headquartered in San Francisco, California, USA, with offices in Dublin, Ireland and Singapore. Our European operations are managed through CloudMetrics Ireland Ltd., registered in Dublin.

## 3. Data We Collect

### 3.1 Customer Account Data
- Name, email address, company name, billing information
- Account credentials and authentication data
- Communication preferences

### 3.2 End-User Analytics Data (collected on behalf of our customers)
- IP addresses (full, unhashed)
- Device fingerprints (browser type, OS, screen resolution, installed fonts, canvas fingerprint)
- Browsing behavior (pages visited, time on page, click patterns, scroll depth)
- Geographic location (city-level, derived from IP)
- Referral sources and search queries
- Custom events defined by our customers

### 3.3 Automated Profiling Data
We create behavioral profiles of end-users by combining:
- Cross-site browsing patterns (across all customer websites using CloudMetrics)
- Purchase history and transaction data
- Demographic inferences (age range, gender, income bracket)
- Interest categories and predictive scores
- Likelihood-to-convert scores

### 3.4 Cookie and Tracking Technologies
- First-party analytics cookies (duration: 2 years)
- Third-party advertising cookies (shared with partners)
- Pixel tracking across email communications
- Local storage for session persistence

## 4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process personal data based on:
- **Legitimate interest** (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR) for analytics processing and profiling
- **Contract performance** (Article 6(1)(b)) for customer account management
- **Consent** for marketing communications

Note: End-user consent for analytics tracking is managed by our customers through their own cookie consent mechanisms. CloudMetrics does not directly obtain consent from end-users.

## 5. Data Sharing and International Transfers

### 5.1 Sub-processors
We share data with the following sub-processors:
- Amazon Web Services (US-East-1 region) — primary data storage
- Google Cloud Platform (US) — backup and disaster recovery
- Snowflake Inc. (US) — data warehousing
- Twilio SendGrid (US) — email communications
- Stripe (US) — payment processing

### 5.2 International Data Transfers
Data collected in the EU/EEA is transferred to the United States for processing. We rely on:
- Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) for EU-US transfers
- Our internal assessment confirms adequate protection measures

### 5.3 Third-Party Data Sharing
We share aggregated and pseudonymized analytics data with:
- Advertising partners for retargeting purposes
- Industry benchmarking services
- Research institutions (academic partnerships)

Note: Pseudonymized data may be re-identified when combined with advertising partner datasets.

## 6. Data Retention

- Customer account data: retained for the duration of the contract plus 7 years
- End-user analytics data: retained for **5 years** from collection date
- Behavioral profiles: retained indefinitely for model training purposes
- Server logs: retained for 24 months
- Backup data: retained for 10 years

## 7. Data Subject Rights

EU/EEA residents have the following rights:
- Right of access (Article 15)
- Right to rectification (Article 16)
- Right to erasure (Article 17)
- Right to restrict processing (Article 18)
- Right to data portability (Article 20)
- Right to object to processing (Article 21)

To exercise these rights, please email privacy@cloudmetrics.io. We will respond within **60 days** of receiving your request.

## 8. Security Measures

- AES-256 encryption at rest
- TLS 1.2+ for data in transit
- SOC 2 Type II certified
- Annual penetration testing
- Access controls and audit logging

## 9. Children's Data

Our service is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect data from children. However, our analytics tracking may inadvertently collect data from children visiting our customers' websites.

## 10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify customers of material changes via email at least 15 days before the changes take effect. Continued use of our services after the effective date constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

## 11. Cookie Consent Mechanism

Our platform provides customers with a JavaScript snippet for cookie consent. The default configuration:
- Pre-checks the "Analytics" checkbox
- Uses a small, dismissible banner (not a blocking modal)
- "Accept All" button is prominently displayed in green
- "Manage Preferences" link is in small gray text
- Scrolling past the banner is treated as implicit consent
- No "Reject All" option is displayed by default

## 12. Automated Decision-Making

Our platform performs automated profiling to generate:
- User engagement scores
- Churn prediction scores
- Lifetime value estimates
- Audience segmentation

These profiles may be used by our customers to make decisions that significantly affect end-users (e.g., pricing, content access, service levels). We have not conducted a Data Protection Impact Assessment for this profiling activity.
