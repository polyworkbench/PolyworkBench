# Rubric: LEG-04

## Dimension 1: GDPR Compliance (Weight: 30%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Matrix identifies consent mechanism violations, Schrems II transfer issues, retention period problems, DPIA requirement, with GDPR article references (3+) and compliance status indicators
- **Good (7-8)**: 3+ violations identified with article references
- **Adequate (5-6)**: 2 violations with some references
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal compliance analysis
- **Zero (0)**: No conformity matrix

## Dimension 2: French Legal Quality (Weight: 25%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Audit report in French with GDPR/data protection terminology (responsable de traitement, base légale, analyse d'impact, CNIL, RGPD), structured with headings, 3000+ chars, formal audit language
- **Good (7-8)**: French report with legal terms and structure
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Some French legal content, 1500+ chars
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal French content
- **Zero (0)**: No French report

## Dimension 3: Risk Identification (Weight: 20%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Risk synthesis identifying 4+ key risks (consent, data transfer, retention, DPIA, minors, response time), severity classification, financial/legal impact mentions (CNIL, amende, 4%)
- **Good (7-8)**: 3+ risks with severity and some impact
- **Adequate (5-6)**: 2 risks identified
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal risk content
- **Zero (0)**: No risk synthesis

## Dimension 4: Action Plan (Weight: 15%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Prioritized actions with timeline/deadlines, specific corrective measures (5+), multiple action items
- **Good (7-8)**: Actions with priority and some timelines
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Basic actions without prioritization
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal plan
- **Zero (0)**: No action plan

## Dimension 5: Cross-Reference (Weight: 10%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: References all sources (English privacy policy/CloudMetrics, CNIL guidance, German court ruling/Landgericht), 5+ GDPR article citations
- **Good (7-8)**: 3 source references with article citations
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Some cross-referencing
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal cross-reference
- **Zero (0)**: No cross-referencing

## Language Quality Assessment
- Target language output must be professional quality French
- No English fallback in audit report and risk synthesis
- Technical terminology must follow French data protection law (CNIL/RGPD) conventions
