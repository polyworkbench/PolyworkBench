# US Sales Tax Rules by State

## Overview
Sales tax in the United States is imposed at the state and local level. There is no federal sales tax. Rates vary significantly by state and locality.

## Applicable States for This Company

### California (CA)
- **State Rate**: 7.25%
- Applies to: Tangible personal property, some digital goods
- Exemptions: Most food for home consumption, prescription medicines
- Note: Local districts may add up to 2.5% additional tax (not applicable for this calculation)

### New York (NY)
- **State Rate**: 8.00% (includes NYC local tax)
- Applies to: Tangible personal property, certain services, digital products
- Exemptions: Clothing under $110, unprepared food, prescription drugs
- Note: 4% state + 4% NYC local = 8% combined rate

### Texas (TX)
- **State Rate**: 6.25%
- Applies to: Tangible personal property, some services
- Exemptions: Food for home consumption, prescription medicines, manufacturing equipment
- Note: Local jurisdictions may add up to 2% (not applicable for this calculation)

## Calculation Rules
- Tax is calculated on the net sale price
- Tax = Net Price × State Rate
- Total = Net Price + Tax
- Rates apply based on ship-to destination (destination-based sourcing)

## Nexus Requirements
- Physical presence in the state creates nexus
- Economic nexus: >$500K revenue or >200 transactions in most states
- Remote sellers must collect if nexus thresholds are met

## Filing Requirements
- Monthly or quarterly filing depending on volume
- Annual reconciliation return
- Separate filing for each state
