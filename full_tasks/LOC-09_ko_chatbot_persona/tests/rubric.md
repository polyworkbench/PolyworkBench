# Rubric: LOC-09

## Dimension 1: Persona Consistency (Weight: 25%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Consistent chatbot personality maintained across all Korean dialogue variations
- **Good (7-8)**: Good consistency with minor variations
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Some persona inconsistencies
- **Poor (1-4)**: Inconsistent persona
- **Zero (0)**: Not attempted or no relevant output

## Dimension 2: Korean Speech Levels (Weight: 25%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Proper Korean speech levels (존댓말/반말/해요체) appropriate to chatbot context and user relationships
- **Good (7-8)**: Good speech level handling
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Basic awareness of speech levels
- **Poor (1-4)**: Inappropriate speech levels
- **Zero (0)**: Not attempted or no relevant output

## Dimension 3: Dialogue Quality (Weight: 20%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Natural Korean conversational dialogue that sounds native (not translated)
- **Good (7-8)**: Good natural Korean with minor issues
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Understandable but clearly translated
- **Poor (1-4)**: Unnatural Korean
- **Zero (0)**: Not attempted or no relevant output

## Dimension 4: Intent Coverage (Weight: 15%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: All chatbot intents and responses covered with appropriate Korean responses
- **Good (7-8)**: Most intents covered
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Partial intent coverage
- **Poor (1-4)**: Major coverage gaps
- **Zero (0)**: Not attempted or no relevant output

## Dimension 5: Cultural Adaptation (Weight: 15%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Korean cultural context reflected (polite forms, cultural references, communication style)
- **Good (7-8)**: Good cultural adaptation
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Basic awareness of cultural differences
- **Poor (1-4)**: No cultural adaptation
- **Zero (0)**: Not attempted or no relevant output

## Language Quality Assessment
- Target language output must be professional quality in Korean
- No unwarranted English fallback (output must be in specified target language: Korean)
- Technical terminology must be domain-appropriate
