# Acme Cloud, Inc. — Privacy Policy (US, CCPA-aligned)

Effective Date: 2024-01-01

## 1. Information We Collect
We collect (a) identifiers (name, email, IP); (b) commercial information (subscription, billing); (c) internet activity (logs, cookies); (d) inferences derived from the above.

## 2. Business Purposes
We use personal information for the following business purposes under CCPA §1798.140(e):
account management, billing, security, analytics, and product improvement.

## 3. Sale or Sharing of Personal Information
We do **not** sell personal information. We share with service providers (subprocessors) under contractual restrictions per CCPA §1798.140(ag).

## 4. Consumer Rights (California Residents)
You have the right to:
(a) know what personal information is collected;
(b) delete personal information (with statutory exceptions);
(c) correct inaccurate personal information;
(d) opt out of sale/sharing (we do not sell, but you may still submit the request);
(e) limit use of sensitive personal information;
(f) non-discrimination for exercising these rights.

To exercise: privacy@acme.com or 1-800-XXX-XXXX. We respond within 45 days.

## 5. Children's Privacy
Our service is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Parental consent is required for users 13–16 in some states.

## 6. Retention
We retain personal information for as long as the account is active, plus 24 months after termination, unless a longer period is required by law (e.g., tax records, 7 years).

## 7. International Transfers
Personal information may be transferred to and processed in the United States. We do not currently offer services subject to GDPR or LFPDPPP. *(Note: this clause is the source of risk for the LATAM expansion.)*

## 8. Security
We implement administrative, technical, and physical safeguards including AES-256 encryption at rest and TLS 1.3 in transit.

## 9. Breach Notification
We will notify affected California residents in the most expedient time possible and without unreasonable delay, consistent with Cal. Civ. Code §1798.82.
