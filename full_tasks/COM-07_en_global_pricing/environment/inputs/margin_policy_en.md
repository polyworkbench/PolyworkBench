# Global Margin Policy

## Overview

This document defines the minimum acceptable margins for all products across international markets. All market pricing must comply with these guidelines.

## Minimum Margin Requirements by Category

| Category | Minimum Gross Margin | Target Margin | Maximum Retail Markup |
|----------|---------------------|---------------|----------------------|
| Wearable Devices | 45% | 55% | 2.5x |
| Audio Equipment | 50% | 60% | 3.0x |
| Charging Accessories | 55% | 65% | 3.5x |
| Health Devices | 50% | 60% | 3.0x |
| Computer Accessories | 50% | 60% | 3.0x |
| Smart Home | 45% | 55% | 2.8x |
| Home Appliances | 45% | 55% | 2.8x |

## Cross-Market Pricing Rules

1. **Price Parity Threshold**: No product should be priced more than 15% above or below the global average (in USD equivalent) without documented justification.

2. **Gray Market Prevention**: Price gaps exceeding 25% between any two markets create arbitrage risk and must be flagged for immediate review.

3. **Local Market Adjustments**: Approved adjustments include:
   - Import duties and taxes (documented per market)
   - Local competitive pressure (requires quarterly review)
   - Purchasing power parity adjustments (max 10%)

4. **Promotional Pricing**: Temporary discounts below minimum margin are allowed for max 14 days per quarter, with pre-approval.

## Margin Calculation

```
Gross Margin = (Retail Price - COGS) / Retail Price × 100%
```

Where COGS includes:
- Factory cost (FOB)
- International shipping (allocated per unit)
- Import duties (market-specific)
- Local warehousing (allocated per unit)

## Compliance

- Monthly automated checks required
- Violations must be resolved within 7 business days
- Repeated violations (3+ in a quarter) trigger pricing committee review

## Effective Date

This policy is effective from October 1, 2025 and supersedes all previous pricing guidelines.
