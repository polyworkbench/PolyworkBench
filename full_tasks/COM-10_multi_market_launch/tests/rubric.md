# Rubric: COM-10

## Dimension 1: Synthesis Quality (Weight: 20%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Market synthesis covering all 4 markets (Japan, Korea, Russia, Vietnam) with quantitative data (20+ numbers), market size, and consumer insights
- **Good (7-8)**: 3+ markets with quantitative data and some insights
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Partial market coverage or limited data
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal synthesis
- **Zero (0)**: No market synthesis

## Dimension 2: Contradiction Detection (Weight: 20%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: 3+ contradictions identified across sources with specific types (market size, growth rates), source attribution, and total count in answer
- **Good (7-8)**: 2+ contradictions with some specifics
- **Adequate (5-6)**: 1 contradiction detected
- **Poor (1-4)**: Vague inconsistency mentions
- **Zero (0)**: No contradiction report

## Dimension 3: Chinese Plan (Weight: 20%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Chinese strategic plan (>30% CJK ratio), 5+ sections, 3000+ chars, covering strategy/pricing/channels/risk for all 4 markets
- **Good (7-8)**: Chinese plan with structure and multi-market coverage
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Chinese content but limited strategy depth
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal Chinese content
- **Zero (0)**: No Chinese plan

## Dimension 4: English Brief (Weight: 15%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: English executive brief (600-2000 words) with summary, recommendations, budget, timeline, and 3+ sections
- **Good (7-8)**: English brief with structure and executive content
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Brief exists but short or poorly structured
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal English brief
- **Zero (0)**: No English brief

## Dimension 5: Timeline Feasibility (Weight: 15%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Timeline with dates for all 4 markets, phases/milestones, and structured data (300+ chars)
- **Good (7-8)**: Timeline with dates and market coverage
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Basic timeline with some dates
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal timeline data
- **Zero (0)**: No timeline

## Language Quality Assessment
- Target language output must be professional quality Chinese (plan) and English (brief)
- No language mixing in designated outputs
- Technical terminology must be domain-appropriate for market launch strategy
