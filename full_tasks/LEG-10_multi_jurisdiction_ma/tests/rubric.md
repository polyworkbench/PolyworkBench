# Rubric: LEG-10

## Dimension 1: Multi-Jurisdiction Analysis (Weight: 30%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Comprehensive M&A review covering all relevant jurisdictions, identifying Korean antitrust trigger, sanctions concerns (Petrov), and valuation gap ($50M vs $38M net assets)
- **Good (7-8)**: Most jurisdictional issues identified
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Some jurisdictional analysis
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal multi-jurisdiction content
- **Zero (0)**: No analysis

## Dimension 2: French Synthesis Quality (Weight: 25%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Professional French memo with proper legal terminology, structured sections, formal register, comprehensive synthesis of findings
- **Good (7-8)**: Good French memo with legal content
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Some French content present
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal French
- **Zero (0)**: No French output

## Dimension 3: Risk Identification (Weight: 20%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: All key risks flagged (antitrust filing, sanctions exposure, valuation discrepancy), with severity assessment and deal-impact analysis
- **Good (7-8)**: 2+ key risks with impact analysis
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Some risks identified
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal risk content
- **Zero (0)**: No risk identification

## Dimension 4: Recommendations (Weight: 15%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Actionable go/no-go recommendation with conditions, timeline for remediation, cost estimates
- **Good (7-8)**: Clear recommendation with some conditions
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Basic recommendation
- **Poor (1-4)**: Vague suggestions
- **Zero (0)**: No recommendations

## Dimension 5: Completeness (Weight: 10%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: All deliverables present, answer.json with correct deal facts and risk assessments
- **Good (7-8)**: Most deliverables present
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Some files missing
- **Poor (1-4)**: Major gaps
- **Zero (0)**: No output

## Language Quality Assessment
- Target language output must be professional quality French
- No English fallback in synthesis memo
- Technical terminology must follow French M&A and corporate law conventions
