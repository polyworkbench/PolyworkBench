### 4. Conditions Precedent

1. **Regulatory Approvals:**
   - China MOFCOM approval (foreign investment)
   - China SAMR merger control clearance
   - Korea KFTC merger notification clearance
   - China cybersecurity/data security review (if triggered)

2. **Corporate Approvals:**
   - Buyer Board Resolution ✅
   - Seller shareholder consent (Chen Wei: conditional on non-compete payment)
   - Horizon Ventures LP Advisory Committee approval

3. **Financial Conditions:**
   - Syndicated loan documentation executed
   - No Material Adverse Change (MAC) in Target

4. **Compliance:**
   - Sanctions clearance for all parties
   - Anti-money laundering checks
   - Tax clearance certificates

### 5. Key Commercial Terms

- **Purchase Price:** USD 50,000,000
- **Price Adjustment:** Net asset-based adjustment at completion (baseline: USD 38M net assets)
- **Escrow:** USD 5,000,000 held in escrow for 18 months (warranty claims)
- **Non-Compete:** Chen Wei subject to 3-year non-compete (geographic: Greater China)
- **Earn-Out:** Additional USD 5,000,000 if FY2024 revenue exceeds RMB 320M
- **Warranties:** Standard business warranties, 18-month survival period
- **Indemnities:** Specific indemnities for tax, IP, data protection matters

### 6. Risk Factors

1. Regulatory timeline risk (multiple jurisdictions)
2. Sanctions compliance (Petrov board membership)
3. China data security review (30% government client revenue)
4. Valuation gap (board approved $50M vs. net assets of $38M)
5. Currency risk (JPY funding for USD-denominated transaction)
6. Chen Wei cooperation risk (key man dependency)
