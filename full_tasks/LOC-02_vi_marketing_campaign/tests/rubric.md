# Rubric: LOC-02

## Dimension 1: Adaptation Quality (Weight: 30%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Creative marketing adaptation (not literal translation) that resonates with Vietnamese audience
- **Good (7-8)**: Good adaptation with creative elements
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Mostly translated rather than adapted
- **Poor (1-4)**: Literal translation or missing
- **Zero (0)**: Not attempted or no relevant output

## Dimension 2: Vietnamese Naturalness (Weight: 25%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Natural Vietnamese with proper diacritics, idiomatic expressions, and engaging marketing tone
- **Good (7-8)**: Good Vietnamese with diacritics and some creativity
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Correct but flat Vietnamese
- **Poor (1-4)**: Poor Vietnamese or missing diacritics
- **Zero (0)**: Not attempted or no relevant output

## Dimension 3: Brand Compliance (Weight: 20%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Full adherence to brand guidelines while adapting for local market
- **Good (7-8)**: Good brand compliance with minor deviations
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Some brand guidelines followed
- **Poor (1-4)**: Brand guidelines ignored
- **Zero (0)**: Not attempted or no relevant output

## Dimension 4: Cultural Fit (Weight: 15%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Culturally appropriate content for Vietnamese market (references, imagery, values)
- **Good (7-8)**: Good cultural adaptation
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Basic awareness of culture
- **Poor (1-4)**: Culturally inappropriate content
- **Zero (0)**: Not attempted or no relevant output

## Dimension 5: Completeness (Weight: 10%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: All campaign materials present (social posts, ad copy, campaign materials, brand report)
- **Good (7-8)**: Most materials present
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Partial materials
- **Poor (1-4)**: Missing most materials
- **Zero (0)**: Not attempted or no relevant output

## Language Quality Assessment
- Target language output must be professional quality in Vietnamese
- No unwarranted English fallback (output must be in specified target language: Vietnamese)
- Technical terminology must be domain-appropriate
