# Rubric: LOC-08

## Dimension 1: Content Accuracy (Weight: 25%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Accurate educational content with correct subject matter preserved in Vietnamese
- **Good (7-8)**: Most content accurately localized
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Some content errors
- **Poor (1-4)**: Major inaccuracies
- **Zero (0)**: Not attempted or no relevant output

## Dimension 2: Vietnamese Pedagogical Quality (Weight: 25%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Natural Vietnamese pedagogical tone with proper diacritics and age-appropriate language
- **Good (7-8)**: Good Vietnamese with pedagogical tone
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Correct but flat Vietnamese
- **Poor (1-4)**: Poor Vietnamese quality
- **Zero (0)**: Not attempted or no relevant output

## Dimension 3: Exercise Quality (Weight: 20%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Well-adapted exercises appropriate for Vietnamese educational context with proper difficulty progression
- **Good (7-8)**: Good exercises with cultural adaptation
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Basic exercise translation
- **Poor (1-4)**: No exercises or poor quality
- **Zero (0)**: Not attempted or no relevant output

## Dimension 4: Standards Alignment (Weight: 15%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Content aligned with Vietnamese educational standards and curriculum requirements
- **Good (7-8)**: Good standards alignment
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Some alignment noted
- **Poor (1-4)**: No standards consideration
- **Zero (0)**: Not attempted or no relevant output

## Dimension 5: Completeness (Weight: 15%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: All required educational materials present and properly structured
- **Good (7-8)**: Most materials present
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Partial materials
- **Poor (1-4)**: Missing most materials
- **Zero (0)**: Not attempted or no relevant output

## Language Quality Assessment
- Target language output must be professional quality in Vietnamese
- No unwarranted English fallback (output must be in specified target language: Vietnamese)
- Technical terminology must be domain-appropriate
