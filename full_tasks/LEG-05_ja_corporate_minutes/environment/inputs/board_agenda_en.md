- Proposal to invest ¥8.5 billion in a new data center in Osaka

- Timeline: Construction Q2 2024 – Q4 2025
- Expected ROI: 12% (5-year projection)
- Capacity: 5,000 server racks
- Sustainability: 100% renewable energy target
- **Proposed Resolution:** Approve capital expenditure of ¥8.5 billion for the Osaka data center project

### Item 6: Appointment of New Independent Director

- Term: 2 years (April 2024 – March 2026)
- Compensation: ¥15 million per year (base) + ¥3 million (committee fees)
- Committees: Audit Committee (Chair), Nomination Committee (Member)
- **Proposed Resolution:** Approve the appointment of Dr. Nakamura Emi as Independent Director effective April 1, 2024
