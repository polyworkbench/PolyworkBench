# Rubric: LEG-05

## Dimension 1: Minutes Format (Weight: 25%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Board minutes with all elements (date/time, location, attendees, agenda items, resolutions, secretary), structured headings, 3000+ chars, 6 agenda items covered
- **Good (7-8)**: 4-5 format elements with structure and most agenda items
- **Adequate (5-6)**: 3 format elements with some coverage
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal format adherence
- **Zero (0)**: No minutes

## Dimension 2: Financial Accuracy (Weight: 25%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Key figures correctly extracted (Q4 revenue 234.7M, FY revenue 856.2M, net profit 98.5M), structured categories (売上, 利益, etc.), Korea subsidiary reference, budget comparison
- **Good (7-8)**: 3+ correct figures with categories
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Some financial data present
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal or inaccurate financials
- **Zero (0)**: No financial summary

## Dimension 3: Japanese Formality (Weight: 20%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: High Japanese ratio (>40%), formal board language (取締役会議事録, 承認, 可決, 全会一致, 審議), polite verb endings, proper titles (代表取締役, 社外取締役)
- **Good (7-8)**: Good Japanese ratio with formal expressions and titles
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Some Japanese formality
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal Japanese or informal
- **Zero (0)**: No Japanese content

## Dimension 4: Resolutions Completeness (Weight: 15%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: 6 resolutions covering all topics (financial approval, business plan, dividend, Korea report, director appointment, share issuance), with vote results
- **Good (7-8)**: 4-5 resolutions with votes and topics
- **Adequate (5-6)**: 2-3 resolutions
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal resolution content
- **Zero (0)**: No resolutions

## Dimension 5: Action Items (Weight: 15%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: 5+ action items with assignees (specific directors), deadlines/dates, and description for each
- **Good (7-8)**: 3+ items with assignees and deadlines
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Some action items
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal actions
- **Zero (0)**: No action items

## Language Quality Assessment
- Target language output must be professional quality Japanese
- No English fallback in board minutes
- Technical terminology must follow Japanese corporate governance and Companies Act conventions
