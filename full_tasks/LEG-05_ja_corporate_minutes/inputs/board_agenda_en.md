**Presenter:** Yamamoto Keiko (CEO)
**Type:** Resolution Item

- Expansion of Korean subsidiary operations
- Investment in quantum computing research partnership

**Proposed Resolution:** Approve the FY2024 business plan and consolidated budget as presented.
