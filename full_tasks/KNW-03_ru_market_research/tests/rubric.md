# Rubric: KNW-03

## Dimension 1: Russian Report Quality (Weight: 25%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Market brief in Russian with professional analysis, data-driven content, structured sections, 2000+ chars
- **Good (7-8)**: Good Russian market brief with data
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Basic Russian report
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal Russian content
- **Zero (0)**: No market brief

## Dimension 2: Multi-Source Integration (Weight: 25%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Data integrated from English (BloombergNEF, CAGR), Chinese (CATL, BYD, GWh), and Korean (LG, Samsung SDI, SK On) sources with specific data points
- **Good (7-8)**: Data from 2+ sources with specific references
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Some cross-source data
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal integration
- **Zero (0)**: No multi-source data

## Dimension 3: Competitor Analysis (Weight: 20%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Detailed competitor analysis in Russian with company profiles, market positioning, and competitive dynamics
- **Good (7-8)**: Good competitor data with Russian content
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Basic competitor information
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal competitor data
- **Zero (0)**: No competitor analysis

## Dimension 4: SWOT Analysis (Weight: 15%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: All 4 SWOT categories (strengths, weaknesses, opportunities, threats) in Russian with 3+ items each, market-relevant content
- **Good (7-8)**: 4 categories with some items in Russian
- **Adequate (5-6)**: 2-3 categories present
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal SWOT content
- **Zero (0)**: No SWOT analysis

## Dimension 5: Market Sizing (Weight: 15%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Market sizing with CAGR, top players, revenue/capacity figures, and growth projections
- **Good (7-8)**: Market sizing with key metrics
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Basic market size data
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal sizing
- **Zero (0)**: No market sizing

## Language Quality Assessment
- Target language output must be professional quality Russian
- No English fallback in market brief and SWOT analysis
- Technical terminology must be domain-appropriate for energy storage market research
