# Global EV Battery Market Analysis Report 2024

## Market Overview

The global electric vehicle (EV) battery market reached $128.7 billion in 2024, representing a year-over-year growth of 23.4% from $104.3 billion in 2023. The market is projected to reach $315.8 billion by 2030, driven by accelerating EV adoption, government mandates for zero-emission vehicles, and continued cost reductions in battery chemistry.

### By Application

- **Passenger EVs**: 72.4% of market ($93.2B)
- **Commercial EVs (buses, trucks)**: 15.8% ($20.3B)
- **Energy Storage Systems (ESS)**: 8.2% ($10.6B)
- **Two/Three-wheelers**: 3.6% ($4.6B)

## Key Market Drivers

1. **Government Policy**: EU ban on ICE vehicles by 2035, US IRA subsidies ($7,500 per EV), China NEV mandate
2. **Cost Reduction**: Battery pack costs declined to $115/kWh in 2024 (from $139/kWh in 2023)
3. **Range Improvement**: Average EV range increased to 380km (from 320km in 2022)
4. **Charging Infrastructure**: Global DC fast chargers grew 45% YoY to 2.1 million units

## Technology Trends

### Near-Term (2024-2026)
- LFP chemistry gaining share due to cost advantage and safety
- Silicon-anode adoption increasing energy density by 15-20%
- Cell-to-Pack (CTP) and Cell-to-Body (CTB) designs eliminating modules

### Mid-Term (2026-2028)
- Semi-solid-state batteries entering mass production (QuantumScape, Toyota targets)
- Sodium-ion batteries for entry-level EVs and ESS
- 800V architecture becoming standard, enabling ultra-fast charging

### Long-Term (2028-2030+)
- All-solid-state batteries achieving commercial viability
- Lithium-sulfur batteries for niche high-energy applications
- Battery-as-a-Service (BaaS) models scaling globally

## Regional Analysis

### China
- Dominates with 65% of global battery production capacity
- CATL and BYD together control 53% of global market
- LFP chemistry preference driven by cost and safety focus
- Government targeting battery recycling mandates by 2025

### South Korea
- Three major players (LG, Samsung SDI, SK On) focused on premium NMC
- Heavy investment in European and North American gigafactories
- Technology leadership in high-nickel cathodes (NCMA, single crystal)

### Europe
- Building domestic capacity through EU Battery Alliance
- Northvolt, ACC (Stellantis/Mercedes), and PowerCo (VW) ramping production
- Targeting 1,000 GWh annual capacity by 2030

### North America
- IRA driving $100B+ in announced battery investments
- Focus on supply chain localization (critical minerals, processing)
- JV model: Korean/Japanese makers partnering with US automakers

## Pricing Outlook

Battery pack price trajectory:
- 2023: $139/kWh
- 2024: $115/kWh
- 2025E: $98/kWh
- 2027E: $78/kWh
- 2030E: $62/kWh

LFP cell prices have reached $53/kWh in China, approaching grid parity for ESS applications.

## Investment Considerations

- **Bull case**: Accelerated EV adoption + ESS demand could push 2030 market to $380B+
- **Bear case**: Economic slowdown, raw material constraints, slower-than-expected solid-state commercialization
- **Key risks**: Geopolitical tensions (US-China), lithium/nickel price volatility, regulatory changes
