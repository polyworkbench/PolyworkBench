# Rubric: MFG-05

## Dimension 1: Risk Identification (Weight: 25%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Risk register with all categories (supply disruption, quality, logistics, financial, geopolitical), likelihood/impact/score elements, single-source risks identified
- **Good (7-8)**: 4+ categories with scoring elements
- **Adequate (5-6)**: 3 categories with some scoring
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal risk identification
- **Zero (0)**: No risk register

## Dimension 2: Supplier Scoring (Weight: 25%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Scorecard with 5-axis evaluation (quality, delivery, cost, flexibility, financial), all 3 countries (CN, KR, VN), correct supplier count (18), quantitative scoring
- **Good (7-8)**: 4+ axes with country coverage and scoring
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Basic scoring with some dimensions
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal scorecard
- **Zero (0)**: No supplier scorecard

## Dimension 3: Japanese Report (Weight: 20%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Rich Japanese content (200+ chars), supply chain terminology (サプライチェーン, リスク, サプライヤー, etc.), 4 report sections, 2000+ chars
- **Good (7-8)**: Good Japanese with terminology and structure
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Some Japanese content with limited structure
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal Japanese content
- **Zero (0)**: No Japanese report

## Dimension 4: Mitigation Quality (Weight: 15%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Japanese mitigation plan with strategies (dual-source, safety stock, monitoring, diversification, contracts), actionable recommendations, 1000+ chars
- **Good (7-8)**: Plan with 3-4 strategies
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Basic mitigation with 2 strategies
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal mitigation content
- **Zero (0)**: No mitigation plan

## Dimension 5: Framework Compliance (Weight: 15%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: ISO 31000 referenced, risk scoring scale (1-5), risk thresholds (low/medium/high/critical), average risk score and highest-risk country identified
- **Good (7-8)**: Framework reference with scoring and some thresholds
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Basic scoring present
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal framework adherence
- **Zero (0)**: No framework compliance

## Language Quality Assessment
- Target language output must be professional quality Japanese
- No English fallback in assessment report and mitigation plan
- Technical terminology must follow Japanese supply chain risk management conventions
