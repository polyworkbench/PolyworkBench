# Rubric: LOC-06

## Dimension 1: Translation Quality (Weight: 25%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Accurate Chinese subtitle translations with proper data JSON, timing report, and terminology glossary
- **Good (7-8)**: Good translations with most supporting files
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Partial translation coverage
- **Poor (1-4)**: Poor or missing translations
- **Zero (0)**: Not attempted or no relevant output

## Dimension 2: Timing Accuracy (Weight: 25%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Correct SRT timing with proper synchronization, appropriate reading speed, and no overlaps
- **Good (7-8)**: Good timing with minor sync issues
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Some timing problems
- **Poor (1-4)**: Major timing errors
- **Zero (0)**: Not attempted or no relevant output

## Dimension 3: Chinese Naturalness (Weight: 20%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Natural Chinese subtitle language with appropriate length constraints and conversational tone
- **Good (7-8)**: Good Chinese with minor unnatural phrasing
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Understandable but unnatural
- **Poor (1-4)**: Poor Chinese quality
- **Zero (0)**: Not attempted or no relevant output

## Dimension 4: Terminology Consistency (Weight: 15%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Consistent technical terminology throughout subtitles aligned with glossary
- **Good (7-8)**: Good consistency with glossary
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Some inconsistencies
- **Poor (1-4)**: Inconsistent terminology
- **Zero (0)**: Not attempted or no relevant output

## Dimension 5: SRT Format Compliance (Weight: 15%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Valid SRT format with correct numbering, timestamps (HH:MM:SS,mmm), and proper encoding
- **Good (7-8)**: Valid format with minor issues
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Parseable but format problems
- **Poor (1-4)**: Invalid SRT format
- **Zero (0)**: Not attempted or no relevant output

## Language Quality Assessment
- Target language output must be professional quality in Chinese
- No unwarranted English fallback (output must be in specified target language: Chinese)
- Technical terminology must be domain-appropriate
