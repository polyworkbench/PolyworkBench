# Video Context: AI Documentary

"The Neural Revolution: How Artificial Intelligence is Reshaping Our World"

## Format
- Duration: ~3 minutes 10 seconds
- Type: Educational documentary
- Target audience: General tech-interested viewers, university level
- Tone: Informative, accessible, slightly formal

## Scene Descriptions

### Segment 1 (00:00:00 - 00:00:30)
**Opening & Introduction**
- Visual: Sweeping shots of modern data centers, circuit board close-ups
- Narrator introduces the documentary topic
- Key concepts: AI overview, machine learning introduction, deep learning basics

**Core Technologies**
- Visual: Animated diagrams of neural network architectures

**Ethics & Safety**

- Keep subtitle rhythm matching the pace of narration
