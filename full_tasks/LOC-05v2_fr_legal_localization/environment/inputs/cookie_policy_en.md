# Cookie Policy

Last updated: March 1, 2024

## 1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files stored on your device. This policy explains our use of cookies in connection with our Service (as defined in our Terms of Service, see terms_of_service, Section 3).[^1]

## 2. Types of Cookies

### 2.1 Essential Cookies
Required for the Service to function. Cannot be disabled.

### 2.2 Analytics Cookies
Help us understand how Users interact with the Service.

### 2.3 Marketing Cookies
Used to deliver relevant advertisements.

## 3. Cookie Management
