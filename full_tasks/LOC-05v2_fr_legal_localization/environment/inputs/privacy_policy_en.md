# Privacy Policy

Effective: March 1, 2024

## 1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and protect your Personal Data (as defined in our Terms of Service, see terms_of_service, Section 3).

## 2. Data Controller

The Controller of your Personal Data is CloudSync SAS, registered in France.[^1]

## 3. Data Collection

We collect the following categories of Personal Data:
- Account information (name, email, phone)
- Usage data (logs, analytics)
- Device information (IP address, browser type)
- Cookies and tracking data (see our Cookie Policy, see cookie_policy)

## 4. Legal Basis

We process your data based on:
- Consent (Article 6(1)(a) GDPR)
- Contractual necessity (Article 6(1)(b) GDPR)
- Legitimate interest (Article 6(1)(f) GDPR)

## 5. Data Subject Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:
- Access your Personal Data
- Rectify inaccurate data
- Erase your data ("right to be forgotten")
- Data portability
- Object to processing

## 6. Data Retention

We retain Personal Data for the duration of the contractual relationship plus 3 years, in accordance with French statute of limitations.[^2]

## 7. International Transfers

Data may be transferred outside the EEA subject to appropriate safeguards (Standard Contractual Clauses).

## 8. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to protect Personal Data, including:
- Encryption at rest (AES-256)
- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.3)
- Access controls and authentication
- Regular security audits
- Employee training on data protection

## 9. Cookies

For information about cookies and tracking technologies, please refer to our Cookie Policy (see cookie_policy).
