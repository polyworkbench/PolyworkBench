# Rubric: LOC-05v2

## Dim 1: Glossary Compliance (30%)
- Excellent: All 20 mandatory terms correctly applied
- Good: 16-19 terms
- Adequate: 10-15 terms
- Poor: <10 terms

## Dim 2: Cross-References (25%)
- Excellent: All cross-document references valid and consistent
- Good: Most references valid
- Adequate: Some valid
- Poor: Broken references

## Dim 3: French Legal Quality (25%)
- Excellent: Professional French legal language
- Good: Acceptable legal French
- Adequate: General French
- Poor: Poor quality

## Dim 4: Footnotes (20%)
- Excellent: All footnotes correctly renumbered and preserved
- Good: Most footnotes correct
- Adequate: Some correct
- Poor: Footnotes missing or broken
