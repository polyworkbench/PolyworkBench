# AMENDMENT NO. 2

## to the Master Agreement RV-2023-0847

**Effective Date: November 15, 2023**

---

This Amendment No. 2 ("Amendment") is entered into by and between:

**TechSolutions GmbH** ("Contractor")  
and  
**GlobalCorp AG** ("Client")

collectively referred to as the "Parties."

---

## RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Parties entered into a Master Agreement dated January 15, 2023 (the "Agreement") and Amendment No. 1 dated June 1, 2023;

WHEREAS, the Parties wish to further amend certain commercial terms of the Agreement;

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

---

## Section 1: Payment Terms (Amending §3 of Master Agreement)

1.1 Section §3(2) of the Master Agreement is hereby amended and restated as follows:

1.2 The Contractor shall submit monthly invoices to the Client. All invoices are due and payable within **net 45 days** from the date of receipt by the Client.

1.3 Early payment discount: A 2% discount shall apply to invoices paid within 15 days of receipt.

1.4 Late payment interest shall accrue at a rate of LIBOR + 3% per annum on any overdue amounts.

1.5 All invoices shall be submitted electronically via the Client's procurement portal and shall include a detailed breakdown of hours worked, rates applied, and deliverables completed.

## Section 2: Termination (Amending §8 of Master Agreement)

2.1 Section §8(1) of the Master Agreement is hereby amended and restated as follows:

2.2 Either Party may terminate this Agreement for convenience by providing **90 days** prior written notice to the other Party.

2.3 Termination notice may be delivered at any time and shall not be restricted to quarter-end dates.

2.4 Upon receipt of a termination notice, the Parties shall jointly develop a transition plan within 30 days to ensure orderly wind-down of ongoing work orders.

2.5 The Contractor shall be entitled to payment for all work performed up to the effective date of termination, including any approved expenses incurred.

## Section 3: Service Level Agreement

3.1 The Contractor shall maintain a minimum system uptime of 99.5% for all managed services.

3.2 Response times for support requests shall be:
- Critical issues: 1 hour
- High priority: 4 hours
- Medium priority: 8 hours
- Low priority: 24 hours

3.3 Failure to meet service levels in any given month shall result in service credits as follows:
- Uptime 99.0-99.4%: 5% credit on monthly fees
- Uptime 98.0-98.9%: 10% credit on monthly fees
- Uptime below 98.0%: 20% credit on monthly fees

## Section 4: Rate Adjustments

4.1 Hourly and daily rates as specified in Appendix 2 of the Master Agreement shall be subject to annual adjustment.

4.2 Rate increases shall not exceed 4% per annum and shall be communicated at least 60 days prior to the start of each contract year.

4.3 Rate adjustments shall be based on the German Consumer Price Index (VPI) published by the Federal Statistical Office.

## Section 5: General Provisions

5.1 Except as expressly modified by this Amendment, all terms and conditions of the Master Agreement and Amendment No. 1 shall remain in full force and effect.

5.2 In the event of any conflict between this Amendment and the Master Agreement or Amendment No. 1, this Amendment shall prevail.

5.3 This Amendment shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the dispute resolution provisions established in Amendment No. 1.
