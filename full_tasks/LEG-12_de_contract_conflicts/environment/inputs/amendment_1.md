# AMENDMENT NO. 1

## to the Master Agreement RV-2023-0847

**Effective Date: June 1, 2023**

---

This Amendment No. 1 ("Amendment") is entered into by and between:

**TechSolutions GmbH** ("Contractor")  
and  
**GlobalCorp AG** ("Client")

collectively referred to as the "Parties."

---

## RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Parties entered into a Master Agreement dated January 15, 2023 (the "Agreement");

WHEREAS, the Parties wish to amend certain provisions of the Agreement to reflect changed business requirements;

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

---

## Section 1: Dispute Resolution (Amending §10 of Master Agreement)

1.1 The Parties agree that Section §10 (Applicable Law and Jurisdiction) of the Master Agreement is hereby amended and replaced in its entirety as follows:

1.2 All disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, including disputes regarding its existence, validity, or termination, shall be resolved by arbitration administered by the **London Court of International Arbitration (LCIA)** in accordance with the LCIA Arbitration Rules in force at the time of filing.

1.3 The arbitral tribunal shall consist of three (3) arbitrators. Each Party shall nominate one arbitrator, and the two Party-nominated arbitrators shall select the presiding arbitrator.

1.4 The seat of arbitration shall be London, United Kingdom.

1.5 The language of the arbitration shall be English.

1.6 The governing law of the Agreement shall remain German law (BGB), but dispute resolution shall be exclusively through LCIA arbitration as specified herein.

## Section 2: Force Majeure (Amending §9 of Master Agreement)

2.1 Section §9(2) of the Master Agreement is hereby amended to clarify the scope of force majeure events.

2.2 The following events shall constitute force majeure:
- Natural disasters (earthquakes, floods, storms, volcanic eruptions)
- Wars, armed conflicts, terrorism, civil unrest
- Strikes and lockouts (excluding internal labor disputes)
- Government orders, embargos, and sanctions
- Critical infrastructure failures (power grid, telecommunications backbone)

2.3 **Expressly excluded** from the definition of force majeure:
- **Pandemics, epidemics, and public health emergencies** (including but not limited to COVID-19 and any future pandemic events)
- Supply chain disruptions not caused by the events listed in 2.2
- Cyberattacks or IT system failures
- Currency fluctuations or economic downturns

2.4 The Parties acknowledge that pandemic events are foreseeable risks that can and should be managed through business continuity planning. Neither Party may invoke pandemic-related circumstances as force majeure to excuse non-performance.

## Section 3: Confidentiality (Amending §5 of Master Agreement)

3.1 Section §5(2) is amended to extend the confidentiality period from five (5) years to seven (7) years following termination of the Agreement.

3.2 The definition of "Confidential Information" is expanded to include all pricing information, staffing details, and project methodologies disclosed during the term of the Agreement.

## Section 4: General Provisions

4.1 Except as expressly modified by this Amendment, all terms and conditions of the Master Agreement shall remain in full force and effect.

4.2 In the event of any conflict between this Amendment and the Master Agreement, this Amendment shall prevail.

4.3 This Amendment may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.
