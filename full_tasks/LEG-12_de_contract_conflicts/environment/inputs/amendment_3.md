# AMENDMENT NO. 3

## to the Master Agreement RV-2023-0847

**Effective Date: March 1, 2024**

---

This Amendment No. 3 ("Amendment") is entered into by and between:

**TechSolutions GmbH** ("Contractor")  
and  
**GlobalCorp AG** ("Client")

collectively referred to as the "Parties."

---

## RECITALS

WHEREAS, the Parties entered into a Master Agreement dated January 15, 2023, Amendment No. 1 dated June 1, 2023, and Amendment No. 2 dated November 15, 2023;

WHEREAS, the Parties wish to address matters of liability and intellectual property rights;

NOW, THEREFORE, the Parties agree as follows:

---

## Section 1: Liability Cap (Amending §7 of Master Agreement)

1.1 Section §7(2) of the Master Agreement is hereby amended and restated in its entirety as follows:

1.2 The aggregate liability of the Contractor under this Agreement, regardless of the form of action or legal theory, shall not exceed **$750,000** (seven hundred fifty thousand US Dollars).

1.3 This liability cap applies to all claims arising under or in connection with this Agreement, including but not limited to:
- Direct damages
- Breach of warranty claims
- Indemnification obligations
- Data breach liabilities

1.4 The liability cap shall be calculated on a rolling 12-month basis, resetting on each anniversary of the Master Agreement.

1.5 Excluded from the liability cap:
- Willful misconduct or gross negligence
- Breach of confidentiality obligations (§5)
- Intellectual property infringement claims
- Death or personal injury caused by negligence

## Section 2: Insurance Requirements

2.1 The Contractor shall maintain the following insurance coverage throughout the term of the Agreement:
- Professional Indemnity Insurance: minimum $2,000,000 per occurrence
- General Liability Insurance: minimum $5,000,000 per occurrence
- Cyber Liability Insurance: minimum $3,000,000 per occurrence

2.2 The Contractor shall provide certificates of insurance upon request and shall notify the Client of any material changes to coverage within 14 days.

## Section 3: Data Protection Addendum

3.1 The Parties acknowledge that the processing of personal data under this Agreement is subject to Regulation (EU) 2016/679 (GDPR) and the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP).

3.2 The Contractor shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures to ensure a level of security appropriate to the risk of processing.

3.3 The Contractor shall not transfer personal data outside the EEA without the Client's prior written consent and appropriate safeguards.

## Section 4: Intellectual Property (Amending §12 of Master Agreement)

4.1 Section §12(1) of the Master Agreement is hereby amended and restated as follows:

4.2 All Work Product (defined as software, documentation, designs, inventions, and other deliverables) created by the Contractor specifically for the Client under this Agreement shall be **jointly owned** by both Parties.

4.3 Joint ownership entails:
- Each Party has the right to use, modify, and sublicense the Work Product without the other Party's consent
- Neither Party may assign its ownership interest without the other Party's written consent
- Revenue generated from third-party licensing of jointly owned Work Product shall be shared equally (50/50)

4.4 Background IP and pre-existing materials remain the sole property of the respective Party.

4.5 Open-source components incorporated into deliverables shall be clearly documented and licensed in accordance with their respective open-source licenses.

## Section 5: Audit Rights

5.1 The Client shall have the right to audit the Contractor's compliance with this Agreement upon 30 days' written notice, no more than once per calendar year.

5.2 Audits shall be conducted during normal business hours and shall not unreasonably interfere with the Contractor's operations.

5.3 The Client shall bear the costs of any audit unless the audit reveals material non-compliance, in which case the Contractor shall bear such costs.

## Section 6: General Provisions

6.1 Except as expressly modified by this Amendment, all terms and conditions of the Master Agreement, Amendment No. 1, and Amendment No. 2 shall remain in full force and effect.

6.2 In the event of any conflict between this Amendment and any prior agreement document, this Amendment shall prevail.

6.3 This Amendment shall be governed by the dispute resolution provisions established in Amendment No. 1.
