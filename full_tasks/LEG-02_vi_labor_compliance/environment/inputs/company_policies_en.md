# Company HR Policies – Viet Tien Electronics Manufacturing Co., Ltd.

## Overview

This document outlines the Human Resources policies of Viet Tien Electronics Manufacturing Co., Ltd. ("the Company") applicable to all employees across all locations in Vietnam.

**Effective Date:** January 1, 2024
**Version:** 3.2
**Approved by:** Board of Directors

---

## 1. Working Hours Policy

### 1.1 Standard Working Hours
- Office staff: 8 hours/day, 5 days/week (Monday–Friday), 40 hours/week
- Factory workers: 8 hours/day, 6 days/week (Monday–Saturday), 48 hours/week
- Shift workers: 8 hours/shift with appropriate rest breaks

### 1.2 Overtime Policy
- Overtime is subject to production requirements and management approval
- Maximum overtime: as required by production demands, not to exceed legal limits
- Overtime compensation: as per Vietnamese law
- During peak production (September–December), extended overtime may be required for factory workers

### 1.3 Night Shift Policy
- Night shift workers (22:00–06:00) receive 30% premium on base salary
- Health checks required annually for night shift workers
- Night shift workers are entitled to a 45-minute break (per Vietnamese law)

## 2. Probation Policy

### 2.1 Probation Duration
- Management positions: up to 180 days
- Professional/technical positions (college degree+): up to 60 days
- Skilled workers/technicians: up to 30 days
- Other positions: up to 6 working days

### 2.2 Probation Salary
- Company standard: 85% of full salary during probation
- Exception: Management positions may be set at 75-85% subject to negotiation

## 3. Leave Policy

### 3.1 Annual Leave
- Standard: 12 working days per year
- Hazardous work: 14 working days per year
- Seniority bonus: +1 day per 5 years of service

### 3.2 Special Leave
- Marriage: 3 days paid
- Child's marriage: 1 day paid
- Bereavement (parent/spouse/child): 3 days paid

## 4. Termination and Severance

### 4.1 Notice Periods
- Company-initiated termination: as per contract type and Vietnamese law
- Employee resignation: as per contract type and Vietnamese law
- Immediate termination: only for serious disciplinary violations as defined by law

### 4.2 Severance Pay
- As required by Vietnamese Labor Code
- Half month salary per year of service for eligible employees

## 5. Disciplinary Procedures

### 5.1 Progressive Discipline
- Step 1: Verbal warning
- Step 2: Written warning (first)
- Step 3: Written warning (final)
- Step 4: Termination

### 5.2 Summary Dismissal
- Only for gross misconduct as defined in Article 125 of the Labor Code
- Requires proper investigation and hearing

## 6. Non-Compete and Confidentiality

### 6.1 Non-Compete
- Applicable to management and key technical positions
- Duration: up to 12 months after termination
- Geographic scope: relevant market area
- Compensation: Monthly payment during restriction period (50% of last monthly salary)

### 6.2 Confidentiality
- All employees must sign confidentiality agreements
- Obligation continues for 2 years after employment ends

## 7. Health and Safety

### 7.1 Occupational Health
- Annual health checks for all employees
- Semi-annual checks for hazardous work positions
- PPE provided free of charge

### 7.2 Workers' Responsibility
- Follow safety procedures
- Report hazards immediately
- Use provided PPE

---

*Note: In case of conflict between this policy and Vietnamese law, Vietnamese law shall prevail.*
