# Thai Duong Manufacturing Co., Ltd. - HR Policies

## Company Overview
Thai Duong Manufacturing Co., Ltd. is a foreign-invested electronics manufacturing company located in Binh Duong Province, Vietnam. The company employs approximately 2,500 workers across three production facilities.

## Working Hours Policy

### Standard Working Hours
- Standard work week: 48 hours (Monday through Saturday)
- Daily hours: 8 hours per day with 30-minute lunch break
- Night shift premium: 30% additional pay

### Overtime Policy
- Overtime is mandatory during peak production periods (Q3-Q4)
- Maximum overtime: 60 hours per month during peak season
- Overtime compensation: 150% for weekdays, 200% for weekends, 300% for holidays
- Employees who refuse overtime during peak season may receive written warnings

### Shift Rotation
- Three-shift rotation system: Morning (6:00-14:00), Afternoon (14:00-22:00), Night (22:00-6:00)
- Shift rotation every 2 weeks
- Minimum rest between shift changes: 8 hours
- Workers must be available for emergency call-backs within 4 hours notice

## Probation Policy

### Probation Periods
- Production workers: 60-90 days
- Technical staff: 60 days
- Supervisors and managers: 120 days
- During probation, salary is 85% of the full position salary

### Probation Evaluation
- Monthly performance review during probation
- Probation may be extended once for up to 30 additional days if performance is borderline

## Leave and Benefits

### Annual Leave
- 12 days per year for standard workers (prorated in first year)
- Additional 1 day per 5 years of service
- Leave must be approved 2 weeks in advance
- Maximum carry-over: 5 days to next year

### Training Bond Policy
- Employees who receive training valued over VND 50,000,000 must sign a service bond
- Bond period: proportional to training cost (1 year per VND 30,000,000)
- Early termination penalty: 100% of training cost repayment

## Young Worker Policy
- Minimum employment age: 15 years
- Workers under 18: restricted hours and work types
- No night work for workers under 18 (company policy states exemptions for "light packaging work")
- Maximum 40 hours per week for workers under 18

## Health and Safety
- Annual health check-up for all workers
- Semi-annual check-up for night shift workers and workers under 18
- Personal protective equipment provided free of charge
- Mandatory safety training before assignment to production floor

## Disciplinary Procedures
- Verbal warning → Written warning → Final warning → Dismissal
- Three late arrivals (>15 min) in one month = 1 written warning
- Refusal to work overtime during peak season = written warning
- Unauthorized absence: immediate suspension pending investigation
