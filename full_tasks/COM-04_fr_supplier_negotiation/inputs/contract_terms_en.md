# Master Purchase Agreement - General Terms and Conditions

## 1. DEFINITIONS AND SCOPE

This Master Purchase Agreement ("MPA") governs all purchase orders ("POs") issued by Buyer to Supplier for goods destined for the European Union market, specifically France.

## 2. PRICING AND PAYMENT TERMS

2.1 **Currency**: All invoices shall be issued in CNY (Chinese Yuan). Payment shall be converted to EUR at the ECB reference rate on the date of payment.

2.2 **Payment Schedule**: Standard terms are 30% advance upon PO confirmation, 70% upon presentation of Bill of Lading (B/L) copies. Alternative: Letter of Credit (L/C) at sight for orders exceeding EUR 50,000.

2.3 **Price Validity**: Quoted prices remain valid for 90 days from the date of quotation. Price adjustments require 60 days written notice.

2.4 **Volume Discounts**:
- 1x MOQ: standard price
- 2x MOQ: 5% discount
- 5x MOQ: 8% discount
- 10x MOQ: 12% discount (negotiable)

## 3. QUALITY ASSURANCE

3.1 **Standards**: All products must comply with applicable EU regulations including CE marking, REACH, RoHS, and sector-specific directives.

3.2 **Inspection**: Buyer reserves the right to conduct pre-shipment inspection (PSI) by an accredited third party (SGS, Bureau Veritas, or TÜV). Cost shared 50/50.

3.3 **Defect Rate**: Acceptable Quality Level (AQL) shall not exceed 2.5% for critical defects, 4.0% for major defects, 6.5% for minor defects per ISO 2859-1.

3.4 **Warranty**: Minimum 12-month warranty from date of delivery to Buyer's warehouse. Supplier shall replace or refund defective goods within 30 days of claim.

## 4. LOGISTICS AND DELIVERY

4.1 **Incoterms**: FOB Shenzhen/Shanghai/Ningbo (port to be specified per PO). Buyer arranges ocean freight.

4.2 **Lead Time**: As quoted, with tolerance of +/- 5 business days. Delay beyond 10 business days entitles Buyer to 1% penalty per week, capped at 10%.

4.3 **Packaging**: Export-standard packaging suitable for ocean freight. Specific packaging requirements per product specification sheet.

4.4 **Documentation**: Commercial Invoice, Packing List, Bill of Lading, Certificate of Origin (Form A for GSP), test reports, CE Declaration of Conformity.

## 5. INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

5.1 Supplier guarantees that products do not infringe any third-party IP rights in the EU territory.

5.2 Custom designs and molds developed for Buyer remain Buyer's exclusive property.

## 6. CONFIDENTIALITY AND EXCLUSIVITY

6.1 Supplier shall not sell identical products to other buyers in France for 12 months from first shipment.

6.2 All product designs, pricing, and commercial terms are confidential.

## 7. FORCE MAJEURE

Standard ICC Force Majeure Clause 2020 applies.

## 8. DISPUTE RESOLUTION

8.1 Governing Law: French law (Code de Commerce)
8.2 Arbitration: ICC Paris, in English language
8.3 Mediation attempted first for disputes under EUR 100,000

## 9. COMPLIANCE

9.1 Anti-corruption: Supplier certifies compliance with French Sapin II law and UK Bribery Act.
9.2 CSR: Supplier shall provide annual social audit report (BSCI or SA8000 equivalent).
9.3 Environmental: Comply with EU packaging and waste directives (94/62/EC).
