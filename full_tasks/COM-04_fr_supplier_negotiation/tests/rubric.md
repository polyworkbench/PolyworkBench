# Rubric: COM-04

## Dimension 1: Memo Quality (Weight: 30%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Professional French negotiation memo with 4+ structured sections, formal business language, deep content covering suppliers, pricing, quality, and strategy
- **Good (7-8)**: French memo with good structure and most negotiation topics covered
- **Adequate (5-6)**: French memo exists but lacks depth or formal tone
- **Poor (1-4)**: Brief memo with minimal French business content
- **Zero (0)**: No memo produced

## Dimension 2: Landed Cost Accuracy (Weight: 25%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: All 8 products with complete cost breakdown (FOB, freight, insurance, duty, transport) in EUR with reasonable values
- **Good (7-8)**: 6+ products with most cost components in EUR
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Some products with partial cost analysis
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal cost data or wrong currency
- **Zero (0)**: No landed cost analysis

## Dimension 3: Counter Proposal (Weight: 20%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: French counter-proposal with negotiation elements (pricing, volume, payment terms, quality guarantees) and trade terms (MOQ, AQL, FOB, Incoterms)
- **Good (7-8)**: Counter-proposal in French with most negotiation and trade elements
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Basic counter-proposal with some elements
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal proposal content
- **Zero (0)**: No counter-proposal

## Dimension 4: Risk Assessment (Weight: 15%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Risk matrix covering currency, delay, quality, supplier dependency with probability/impact scores and mitigation measures
- **Good (7-8)**: Risk matrix with most categories and scoring elements
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Basic risk identification without full scoring
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal risk content
- **Zero (0)**: No risk matrix

## Dimension 5: French Professionalism (Weight: 10%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Business French terminology (chiffre d'affaires, coût débarqué, cahier des charges) used correctly across all documents
- **Good (7-8)**: Good French business vocabulary in most documents
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Adequate French but limited business terminology
- **Poor (1-4)**: Basic French with no professional vocabulary
- **Zero (0)**: No French content

## Language Quality Assessment
- Target language output must be professional quality French
- No English fallback in memo or counter-proposal
- Technical terminology must follow French international trade conventions
