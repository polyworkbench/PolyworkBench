## Article 1 – Definitions and Interpretation

1.1 In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires:

- "Contract Price" means the total price for the Goods as set out in Schedule A, exclusive of taxes, duties, and shipping costs.
- "Delivery Date" means the date specified in the Purchase Order for delivery of the Goods.
- "Force Majeure" means any event beyond the reasonable control of a party, including but not limited to natural disasters, war, government action, pandemic, or supply chain disruption.
- "Goods" means the electronic components and finished products described in Schedule A.
- "Incoterms" means the International Commercial Terms published by ICC, 2020 edition.
- "Intellectual Property" means all patents, trademarks, copyrights, trade secrets, and know-how related to the Goods.

1.2 The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect interpretation.

1.3 References to statutes include amendments and re-enactments thereof.

## Article 2 – Scope of Supply

2.1 The Seller agrees to manufacture and supply the Goods to the Buyer in accordance with the specifications set out in Schedule A.

2.2 The Goods shall conform to the quality standards specified in Schedule B (Quality Requirements).

2.3 The Seller shall maintain ISO 9001:2015 certification throughout the term of this Agreement.

---

## Article 3 – Price and Payment Terms

3.1 The Contract Price for the initial order shall be USD 4,500,000 (Four Million Five Hundred Thousand US Dollars), subject to adjustment as per Article 3.3.

3.2 **Payment Terms:** The Buyer shall pay the Seller within **sixty (60) days** from the date of receipt of a valid invoice and conforming delivery documentation, by wire transfer to the Seller's designated bank account.

3.3 Price adjustments shall be permitted annually, not exceeding 5% of the previous year's Contract Price, subject to 90 days' written notice.

3.4 Late payments shall bear interest at the rate of 2% per annum above the Bank of England base rate.

3.5 All payments shall be made in US Dollars unless otherwise agreed in writing.

---

## Article 4 – Delivery

4.1 Delivery shall be made CIF London (Incoterms 2020) unless otherwise specified in the Purchase Order.

4.2 The Seller shall deliver the Goods within 45 calendar days from receipt of a confirmed Purchase Order.

4.3 Partial deliveries are permitted with the prior written consent of the Buyer.

4.4 Risk in the Goods shall pass to the Buyer upon delivery as defined in the applicable Incoterms.

---

## Article 5 – Quality and Inspection

5.1 The Buyer shall have thirty (30) days from delivery to inspect the Goods and notify the Seller of any defects or non-conformity.

5.2 Defective Goods shall be replaced or repaired at the Seller's expense within 21 days of notification.

5.3 The Seller warrants that the Goods shall be free from defects in materials and workmanship for a period of twenty-four (24) months from delivery.

---

## Article 6 – Liability and Indemnification

6.1 **Liability Cap:** The Seller's total aggregate liability under this Agreement shall not exceed **Five Million Chinese Yuan (CNY 5,000,000)**, equivalent to the Contract Price ceiling for any single calendar year.

6.2 Neither party shall be liable for indirect, consequential, or punitive damages, including but not limited to lost profits or business interruption.

6.3 The Seller shall indemnify the Buyer against all claims, losses, and expenses arising from:
   (a) defects in the Goods;
   (b) infringement of third-party intellectual property rights;
   (c) breach of applicable laws or regulations.

6.4 The indemnification obligations shall survive termination of this Agreement for a period of three (3) years.

---

## Article 7 – Intellectual Property

7.1 All Intellectual Property in the Goods developed specifically for the Buyer shall vest in the Buyer upon payment of the Contract Price.

7.2 The Seller retains ownership of all pre-existing Intellectual Property and grants the Buyer a non-exclusive, royalty-free license to use such IP solely in connection with the Goods.

7.3 The Seller warrants that the Goods do not infringe any third-party intellectual property rights.

---

## Article 8 – Confidentiality

8.1 Each party shall maintain the confidentiality of all Confidential Information received from the other party for a period of five (5) years from the date of disclosure.

8.2 Confidential Information may be disclosed to employees and professional advisors on a need-to-know basis, subject to equivalent confidentiality obligations.

---

## Article 9 – Term and Termination

9.1 This Agreement shall commence on the date first written above and continue for an initial term of three (3) years, unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Article.

9.2 Either party may terminate this Agreement:
   (a) by giving six (6) months' written notice;
   (b) immediately upon material breach by the other party that remains uncured for 30 days after written notice;
   (c) immediately upon the insolvency or bankruptcy of the other party.

9.3 Upon termination, all outstanding Purchase Orders shall be fulfilled unless otherwise agreed.

---

## Article 10 – Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

10.1 **Governing Law:** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the **laws of England and Wales**.

10.2 **Dispute Resolution:** Any dispute arising out of or in connection with this Agreement shall be referred to and finally resolved by arbitration under the Rules of the London Court of International Arbitration (LCIA).

10.3 The seat of arbitration shall be London, England.

10.4 The language of arbitration shall be English.

10.5 The arbitral tribunal shall consist of three (3) arbitrators.

---

## Article 11 – General Provisions

11.1 This Agreement, together with all Schedules and any side letters executed by both parties, constitutes the entire agreement between the parties.

11.2 No amendment to this Agreement shall be effective unless made in writing and signed by both parties.

11.3 In the event of conflict between this Agreement and any side letter or supplementary document, this Agreement shall prevail unless the side letter expressly states otherwise with reference to the specific Article being modified.

11.4 This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original.
