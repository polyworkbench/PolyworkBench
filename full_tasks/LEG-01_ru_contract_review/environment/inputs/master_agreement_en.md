# MASTER PURCHASE AGREEMENT

**Agreement No.: MPA-2024-0892**

**Date:** January 15, 2024

## PARTIES

This Master Purchase Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between:

**BUYER:** TechGlobal Solutions Ltd., a company incorporated under the laws of England and Wales, with registered office at 45 Canary Wharf, London E14 5AB, United Kingdom ("Buyer")

**SELLER:** 深圳华创科技有限公司 (Shenzhen HuaChuang Technology Co., Ltd.), a company incorporated under the laws of the People's Republic of China, with registered office at Building 8, Nanshan Science Park, Shenzhen 518057, China ("Seller")

---

## ARTICLE 1: DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION

1.1 "Goods" means electronic components, semiconductor devices, and related materials as specified in Purchase Orders issued under this Agreement.

1.2 "Purchase Order" means a written order issued by Buyer to Seller for the supply of Goods.

1.3 "Delivery Date" means the date specified in each Purchase Order for delivery of Goods.

1.4 "Contract Price" means the price for Goods as agreed in each Purchase Order.

## ARTICLE 2: SCOPE AND DURATION

2.1 This Agreement establishes the general terms and conditions governing the purchase and sale of Goods between the Parties.

2.2 This Agreement shall be effective from the date first written above and shall remain in force for a period of three (3) years unless terminated earlier in accordance with Article 12.

2.3 Individual Purchase Orders shall be governed by this Agreement and shall incorporate these terms by reference.

## ARTICLE 3: ORDERS AND ACCEPTANCE

3.1 Buyer may issue Purchase Orders to Seller from time to time during the term of this Agreement.

3.2 Seller shall confirm acceptance or rejection of each Purchase Order within five (5) business days of receipt.

3.3 Failure to respond within the specified period shall be deemed acceptance of the Purchase Order.

## ARTICLE 4: PRICE AND PAYMENT

4.1 Prices shall be as set forth in each Purchase Order and shall be denominated in United States Dollars (USD).

4.2 **Payment Terms:** Payment shall be made within thirty (30) days of receipt of a valid invoice and conforming delivery of Goods ("Net 30").

4.3 Invoices shall be submitted electronically to accounts@techglobal.co.uk and shall include: Purchase Order number, description of Goods, quantities, unit prices, and total amount.

4.4 Late payments shall accrue interest at a rate of 1.5% per month or the maximum rate permitted by law, whichever is lower.

4.5 Buyer may withhold payment for Goods that do not conform to specifications without waiving any other rights or remedies.

## ARTICLE 5: DELIVERY

5.1 Delivery shall be made DDP (Delivered Duty Paid, Incoterms 2020) to Buyer's designated warehouse in the United Kingdom.

5.2 Time is of the essence with respect to delivery. Seller shall deliver Goods on or before the Delivery Date specified in each Purchase Order.

5.3 Seller shall provide advance shipping notices at least five (5) business days prior to shipment.

## ARTICLE 6: QUALITY AND INSPECTION

6.1 All Goods shall conform to the specifications, drawings, and quality standards set forth in the applicable Purchase Order.

6.2 Buyer shall have thirty (30) days from receipt of Goods to inspect and either accept or reject the Goods.

6.3 Rejected Goods shall be returned to Seller at Seller's expense, and Seller shall provide replacement Goods within fifteen (15) business days.

## ARTICLE 7: WARRANTIES

7.1 Seller warrants that all Goods shall be free from defects in materials and workmanship for a period of twenty-four (24) months from the date of delivery.

7.2 Seller warrants that all Goods shall conform to applicable specifications and shall be fit for their intended purpose.

7.3 Seller warrants that the manufacture, sale, and use of Goods shall not infringe any third-party intellectual property rights.

## ARTICLE 8: LIABILITY AND INDEMNIFICATION

8.1 **Liability Cap:** The total aggregate liability of either Party under this Agreement shall not exceed Five Million Chinese Yuan (CNY 5,000,000) or the equivalent in USD.

8.2 Neither Party shall be liable for indirect, consequential, incidental, or punitive damages.

8.3 Seller shall indemnify and hold harmless Buyer from any claims, damages, or losses arising from: (a) defective Goods; (b) breach of warranty; (c) infringement of intellectual property rights.

8.4 The limitations in this Article 8 shall not apply to: (a) fraud or willful misconduct; (b) death or personal injury; (c) breach of confidentiality obligations.

## ARTICLE 9: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

9.1 All intellectual property rights in designs, specifications, and drawings provided by Buyer shall remain the property of Buyer.

9.2 Seller shall not use Buyer's intellectual property for any purpose other than the performance of this Agreement.

## ARTICLE 10: CONFIDENTIALITY

10.1 Each Party shall maintain the confidentiality of all Confidential Information received from the other Party for a period of five (5) years following termination of this Agreement.

10.2 "Confidential Information" includes but is not limited to: pricing, technical specifications, business plans, customer lists, and financial information.

## ARTICLE 11: FORCE MAJEURE

11.1 Neither Party shall be liable for failure to perform obligations due to Force Majeure events, provided that the affected Party gives prompt written notice.

11.2 If a Force Majeure event continues for more than ninety (90) days, either Party may terminate this Agreement by written notice.

## ARTICLE 12: TERMINATION

12.1 Either Party may terminate this Agreement by giving six (6) months' written notice to the other Party.

12.2 Either Party may terminate immediately upon material breach by the other Party that remains uncured for thirty (30) days after written notice.

12.3 Upon termination, all outstanding Purchase Orders shall be fulfilled unless otherwise agreed in writing.

## ARTICLE 13: GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTE RESOLUTION

13.1 **Governing Law:** This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of England and Wales.

13.2 **Jurisdiction:** The courts of England and Wales shall have exclusive jurisdiction to settle any disputes arising out of or in connection with this Agreement.

13.3 **Arbitration Option:** Notwithstanding Article 13.2, the Parties may by mutual written agreement submit any dispute to arbitration under the ICC Rules, with the seat of arbitration in London.

## ARTICLE 14: GENERAL PROVISIONS

14.1 **Entire Agreement:** This Agreement, together with all Purchase Orders and attachments, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties.

14.2 **Amendments:** No amendment to this Agreement shall be effective unless made in writing and signed by both Parties.

14.3 **Severability:** If any provision is held invalid, the remaining provisions shall continue in full force and effect.

14.4 **Language:** This Agreement is executed in English, which shall be the controlling language for all purposes.

14.5 **Notices:** All notices shall be in writing and delivered to the addresses set forth above.
