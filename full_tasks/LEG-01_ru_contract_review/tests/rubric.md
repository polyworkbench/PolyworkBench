# Rubric: LEG-01

## Dimension 1: Conflict Detection (Weight: 30%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: 3+ conflicts identified including payment terms (30 vs 60 days), liability cap (5M vs 10M CNY), and jurisdiction (England vs China/CIETAC vs ICC)
- **Good (7-8)**: 2+ conflicts with specific clause references
- **Adequate (5-6)**: 1-2 conflicts detected without full specifics
- **Poor (1-4)**: Vague conflict mentions
- **Zero (0)**: No conflict analysis

## Dimension 2: Russian Legal Quality (Weight: 25%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Legal memo in Russian (>50% Cyrillic), with legal terminology (юридический, договор, обязательство, ответственность), structured headings, 2000+ chars
- **Good (7-8)**: Russian memo with legal terms and structure
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Some Russian legal content, 1000+ chars
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal Russian content
- **Zero (0)**: No Russian memo

## Dimension 3: Risk Assessment (Weight: 20%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Risk register with severity classifications (including high/critical), required fields (id, description, severity), clause references, structured entries
- **Good (7-8)**: Risks with severity and some clause references
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Basic risk entries without full classification
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal risk data
- **Zero (0)**: No risk register

## Dimension 4: Recommendations (Weight: 15%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: Russian recommendations (>40% Cyrillic), 500+ chars, actionable language (рекомендуем, необходимо, следует), specific resolution suggestions
- **Good (7-8)**: Russian recommendations with actionable content
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Some recommendations present
- **Poor (1-4)**: Minimal recommendation content
- **Zero (0)**: No recommendations

## Dimension 5: Completeness (Weight: 10%)
- **Excellent (9-10)**: All 4 output files present, answer.json with total_conflicts (>=3), high_risk_count, and jurisdiction_conflict fields
- **Good (7-8)**: Most files present with answer.json
- **Adequate (5-6)**: Some files present
- **Poor (1-4)**: Major deliverables missing
- **Zero (0)**: No output

## Language Quality Assessment
- Target language output must be professional quality Russian
- No English fallback in legal memo and recommendations
- Technical terminology must follow Russian commercial law conventions
