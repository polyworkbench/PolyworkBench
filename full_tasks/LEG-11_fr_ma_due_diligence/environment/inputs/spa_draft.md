# SHARE PURCHASE AGREEMENT (Draft v0.3)

**This Agreement** is made on [●], 2024, by and between:

(1) **LuxParis Holdings S.A.**, incorporated under the laws of France ("Purchaser"); and
(2) **Pacific Capital Partners L.P.**, a Cayman Islands limited partnership ("Seller"),

(collectively, the "Parties").

## 1. Definitions

"Target" means AsiaCommerce Holdings Pte. Ltd., a Singapore private limited company, holding 100% of the equity in operating subsidiaries in the PRC, Japan, and the Republic of Korea.

"Closing" means the consummation of the transactions contemplated herein.

"Material Adverse Effect" or "MAE" means any change resulting in a decline of more than 15% in the Target's consolidated EBITDA on a trailing-twelve-month basis.

## 2. Sale and Purchase

The Seller agrees to sell, and the Purchaser agrees to purchase, 100% of the issued and outstanding shares of the Target for a base consideration of **EUR 280,000,000**, subject to adjustment per Section 3.

## 3. Purchase Price Adjustment

Net working capital target: EUR 18,000,000. Adjustment euro-for-euro at Closing.

## 4. Conditions Precedent (CPs)

(a) Antitrust clearances in the EU, PRC (SAMR), and Japan (JFTC).
(b) Absence of MAE between signing and Closing.
(c) Receipt of consents from key customers representing ≥80% of FY2023 revenue.
(d) Delivery of audited 2023 financial statements within 30 days of signing.
(e) **Resolution of pending litigation in the Republic of Korea** (the "Korean Tax Matter").

## 5. Representations and Warranties

The Seller represents and warrants that:
(a) the Target has been duly organized and is validly existing;
(b) the financial statements fairly present the Target's financial condition;
(c) **no material undisclosed liabilities** exceeding EUR 500,000 individually;
(d) all taxes have been duly paid (with carve-out for the Korean Tax Matter);
(e) compliance with applicable data protection laws (PIPL, APPI, PIPA).

## 6. Indemnification

Cap: 25% of the purchase price (i.e., EUR 70,000,000).
Basket (de minimis): EUR 100,000 per claim, EUR 1,000,000 aggregate.
Survival: 24 months post-Closing for general warranties; 7 years for tax warranties.

## 7. Governing Law and Dispute Resolution

This Agreement shall be governed by **English law**.
Disputes shall be submitted to **arbitration under the ICC Rules**, seat: Singapore, language: English. **Singapore International Arbitration Centre (SIAC) supplemental procedures apply.**

## 8. Confidentiality and Public Announcements

No public announcement until Closing without mutual written consent.
